**Jean** is the elf hunting [[Aurelia]] for the crown of [[Ouranoú]]. He is handsome, quiet, and very good at his work, and he carries an old scar on his left shoulder. To the party he looks like an assassin with a job. The truth is worse, and softer: **Jean is the person Aurelia left behind.**

*Model the relationship on **She-Ra and Catra**: two who grew up as each other's whole world, split when one of them "left," love gone bitter and obsessive, and a long road back that neither will admit they want.*

# The Bond
Aurelia grew up a princess in [[Tārā]]. Jean grew up at her side, the overlooked one, lowborn or wardless, always half a step behind her destiny and her title. They were inseparable, each other's only real person. Whatever Aurelia's flirtation is now (armor she uses on everyone), with Jean it was once the genuine thing, and she buried it.

# The Leaving
When [[Queen Amanté of Ouranoú|Queen Amanté]] framed Aurelia for the king's murder, Aurelia fled into exile to survive. She ran, suddenly, to save her own life, and she did not, could not, take Jean. From her side it was survival. From his side it was the one wound that never closed: *she left him.*

%%
**The Rise (GM).** Jean stayed. To survive the Queen's court (and, he tells himself, to be the one who finds Aurelia), he rose into her service as an elite blade, "the Silent Princess." The Queen knows exactly what she is doing: she sends **the one person who loves Aurelia most** to kill her. His hurt is her leash.

**The Scar.** His left shoulder is the Queen's mark: her punishment the first time Aurelia slipped the net, a reminder of what his loyalty is worth. He blames Aurelia for the pain her leaving cost him. He is wrong, and half-knows it.
%%

# The Hunt Now
Duty is the mask; he cannot let her go. The [[Silent Princess]] flower he left at the inn was not a calling card, it was a message only she would read. He resents the [[Boneshield Misfits]].

Aurelia made a **new family** and looks happy without him. Now the [[Crooked Grasp]] has trapped him in the valley too, so he cannot finish the job and vanish. He is stuck sharing her world, which is exactly what breaks people like him open.

# Personality
- Fights as a love language. He picks at her, tests her, keeps *not* landing the killing blow.
- Wants, more than the kill, for her to say she is sorry, that he mattered, that she thinks about him.
- Cold and recise in front of others; only Aurelia can crack the composure.

# Flipping Him (GM)
He believes the Queen's story: that Aurelia betrayed the kingdom and abandoned him. The lever is **truth**. If Aurelia claims the **[[Seelie Court Jewel]]** (glows near lies), she can prove the Queen lied to *both* of them, which is the thing that could break the leash and turn him, the "Catra turn."

**Outcomes (open):**
- **Recurring rival** who now knows the party and keeps coming back.
- **Fragile ally** who defects for her and hates himself for it at first.
- **Tragedy** if the truth comes too late.

Romance vs. fierce found-family love: leave it ambiguous and let the table pull. The ache is the point either way.
