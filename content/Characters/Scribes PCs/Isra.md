*[[Dreamers|Dreamer]] Race, Eldritch Cleric, Amnesiac ([[Thread of the Apocalypse]])*

Knowledge Keeper of a long lost race.

Awakening from a stasis pod deep in a [[Dreamcaves|Dreamcave]] within a nearby mine, with no memory of their past, this '**Dreamer**' met a lone dwarf who grounded them in the present year just before they emerged into the waking nightmare. Isra has little memories, but she is determined to regain them in this new world. 

She travels with the [[Boneshield Misfits]].

# Personality
Isra has a strong faith with their Eldritch Deity (God of Secrets and Knowledge)
