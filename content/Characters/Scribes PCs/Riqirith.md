*Eladrin [[Elf]], Mutation Druid, [[Thread of Rejuvenation]]*

The '**Wild-Witch of Gul'**, as the residents of [[Boneshield]] call her, has long been the protector of its nearby forests. 

The forests, however, were her playground for experimentation on the local flora and fauna - for a greater cause, to help the animals and plants adapt to a world that wasn’t made for them, she gives them new powers and evolves them beyond their natural forms.  

She has a pet Displacer Kitten of her own creation named **Shifty**.

She now travels with the [[Boneshield Misfits]].
