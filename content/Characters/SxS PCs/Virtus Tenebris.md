# Virtus Tenebris

## **Background.** 
A female warrior dedicated to protecting her friends and family. Virtus comes from a pedigreed lineage of famous warriors and explorers known as the [[Tenebris Estate|Tenebris family]]. Virtus demonstrated from a very young age her leadership capabilities and effortlessly rose through the ranks of the [[Solari Crusader|Solari Crusaders]], quickly being chosen to become a [[Solar Scions|Solar Scion]] shortly after.

[[Amiel Tenebris|Sir Tenebris]], a [[Solar Scions|Solar Scion]] and her father, went missing ten years ago in an expedition to find [[Paradise]]; this event ignited Virtus and her brother [[Animus Tenebris|Animus]] to become Solar Scions and explore the world like their father before them. Despite the absence of her father, Virtus respects her mother [[Zariel Tenebris]], Lord Commander of [[Astraloftum]], for defending the floating capital of the [[Ecclesia Solari]]. Her brother [[Animus Tenebris|Animus]] is a famous and seasoned [[Solar Scions|Solar Scion]] that she shares a fierce rivalry with.

Virtus ascended to the [[Solar Scions]] and now travels with her group as its leader and shield-maiden. Virtus thinks a fight is the easiest solution to a problem, and the most clear-cut resolution. But she'd rather not risk her team and resources every time the Scions meet some disrespectful punks.

## **Key Moments & Growth.**

- Is childhood friends with [[Diallos]] and [[Pinot]].
- Despises [[Vincent of Damocles]].
- Received a **Mirror Tower Shield** from the great Golem guardian inside the [[Great Natu Tree]] as a reward for the Scions' bravery.
- While training at the [[Skysmith Forge]], Virtus began forging mithril chains under [[Hemdall Skysmith II]]'s tutelage.
- Was buried alive under an avalanche triggered by [[Air Hjorgin Stormson]] during the battle over the Theus Skyfjords — [[Silas Clemens|Silas]] dug her out with his bare hands. She emerged with a **permanent facial scar** from the crushing ice and led the Scions to victory regardless.
- **Forged her own Adamantine Plate armor** from the Adamantine ore recovered during the battle, during the rest period at [[Kelda-Dhrum|Sul Kelda-Dhrum]].
- Acquired a **Glide Cloak** in [[Janus City]] during the Saturn arc.
- Won a fistfight against [[Thirteen]] outside the Skysmith Forge after confronting him over his dual loyalty to [[High Priestess Cetra]] — her anger wasn't at his past, but at his ongoing secrecy. 

**The Tenebris Estate.**
Returned to find her childhood home transformed into a Solari Crusader training academy under [[Vincent of Damocles]]'s direction. Confronted her mother [[Zariel Tenebris]] directly, accusing her of being "malleable" under Vincent's influence — and struck back with the devastating truth that Vincent had auctioned off their father's Holy Chest to pirates in Janus City. In a rare moment of vulnerability, Virtus appealed not to the General but to her mother, and Zariel responded in kind. Zariel gave Virtus the **Tenebris Master Key** and arranged a decoy meeting with Vincent to allow the Scions to raid his study.

**The Midnight Heist.**
Led the Soul Shepherds into Vincent's private study, where they uncovered: a ledger of illegal estate sales, a chilling **Death Note** of names marked 'H' (Harvested?) or 'P' (Pending?). Every Soul Shepherd is marked 'P'; Zariel, Pinot, and Father Ghaspen are marked 'H'. Armed with this evidence, Zariel began formulating a plan to excise Vincent from the Tenebris legacy. Virtus has since handed the Death Note and ledger to [[Zariel Tenebris]] for her mother to use politically.

**Amiel's Trail — Active Lead.**
- [[Vincent of Damocles]] claimed "a clue to Paradise is hidden inside the [[Project A.R.K.|airship]]" — context unknown, but potentially tied to Amiel's last expedition notes.
- [[Animus Tenebris|Animus]] mentioned that Amiel had a close friend on [[Venus|Aphra]] — but only women may enter.
- The [[J.T. Holy Chest|J.T. Holy Mahogany Chest]] — her father's sacred heirloom — was successfully recovered from the Cloudbeard Pirates and is now aboard the A.R.K.

**Jupiter — Current Events.**
- Stood by [[Silas Clemens|Silas]]'s side with a trusting gaze during the Forvivlad bloodletting procedure on [[Ribny]], prepared to strike Silas down only if he succumbed to his vampiric nature. He didn't.
- Participated with [[Thirteen]] and [[Joy]] in questioning [[Editor Rupert]] in the War Room, listening to the Ink-Radio broadcast of [[Traitor King Dumac]].
- Pressed Rupert on the rumors of [[King Dumaclath]] potentially still being alive — Rupert acknowledged the rumors but said he hasn't devoted resources to investigating.
