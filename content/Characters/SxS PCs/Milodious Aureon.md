# Milodius Aureon
*[[Titanborn]] — [[Children of Jas]], College of Valor Bard, Wandering Minstrel*

Milo is 24, an adult Titanborn birdfolk that hails from the [[Titan]] [[Saturn|Janus]]. A graduate of the prestigious [[College of Damocles]], Milo's passion for astronomy is his map. He navigates by the celestial ley-lines of the [[Skysea]] and composes the rise and fall of kingdoms, the romance of forgotten lords, and the gritty folk tales of the common folk.

Signature Shanty — **"[[Bigby's Whiskey]]"**

There is method to his madness; he puts on a facade of eccentricity to disarm people. Despite this, he is a gregarious individual who enjoys socializing with everyone. A brave and selfless [[Titanborn]] full of compassion, Milo returns to [[Saturn]] frequently. His family believes he is just a wandering singer — they have no idea he is a "Wandering Hero" who has faced various horrors of the [[Skysea]].

Milo carries a **Lute of Illusions** that he uses to add illusory flair to his songs.

---

## **Joining the Soul Shepherds.**
Milo was discovered by the Soul Shepherds aboard the enemy vessel of Stone Giant Corsair [[Gunhild Stormson]], where he had been held captive and forced to play battle music for the giants. He was imprisoned alongside [[Joy]] and helped her attempt to escape during the Soul Shepherds' ambush over the Theus Skyfjords. He used *Polymorph* to transform Gunhild into a turtle during the final stages of the battle, turning the tide of the fight. He formally introduced himself to the Scions afterward and has joined the [[Soul Shepherds]] in liberating [[Jupiter]] from [[Cloudbeard]].

## **Notable Moments.**
- His *Polymorph* spell on [[Gunhild Stormson]] ended the battle at the Theus Skyfjords.
- Escaped from a locked cage below Gunhild's ship — his cage was destroyed by one of [[Silas Clemens|Silas]]'s Lightning Bolts — and fought alongside [[Joy]] during the chaos.
- Sang a booming shanty during the desperate passage through the [[Gales of Ikaros]], staving off the magical lethargy of a freezing slumber as the A.R.K. pushed through Cloudbeard's storm.
- Made his formal introductions to the party during the recovery period after the Theus Skyfjords battle, while [[Joy]] was healing in the A.R.K.'s clinic.
- Talked with Joy about the resistance during the moss-covered scaffolding climb up to the [[Inverted Temple]], sharing stories of what he has witnessed so far.
- At the [[Inverted Temple]] base camp, performed a massive Unity Healing spell alongside [[Silas Clemens|Silas]], spreading magical healing to all of the wounded [[Bhan-Ember]] resistance fighters — saving many lives and gaining significant favor with the resistance.
- Was disturbed by [[Silas Clemens|Silas]] absorbing the Souls of the Dwarven Kings in the [[Deep Hall of the Regents]], though he pushed past his discomfort and did not intervene.

## **Personality & Role.**
Milo brings a natural warmth and camaraderie to the group — a troubadour's instinct to witness and document the weight of what is happening around him. He shares a natural rapport with [[Joy]], and acts as a living witness to the resistance's struggle on Jupiter, documenting it in the tradition of a troubadour. He is still new to the group's internal dynamics and is finding his footing.

## **Family & Saturn.**
His family on Saturn has no idea of the dangers he regularly faces. They believe he is a wandering singer. He is a Child of Jas — a Titanborn of [[Saturn|Janus]] — and his homeland's culture and ley-line navigation inform the way he moves through the world. The 'Mad Shamaness' [[Jaz'Farrah]] is his cousin.

## Personal Quests.
**Chronicling Jupiter** — Milo documents the struggle of the [[Bhan-Ember]] and the [[Scribes]] as history being made. He intends this to become part of the folk record — songs and verse that will outlast the revolution.
