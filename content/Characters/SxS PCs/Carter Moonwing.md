# Carter Moonwing

## **Background.** 
Esteemed [[Solar Scions|Solar Scion]] Carter Moonwing is a young Owlin ([[Children of Jas|Child of Jas]]) wizard who graduated at the top of his class in [[College of Damocles]] under the tutelage of the wizened [[Professor Gobby]]. Carter suffers from a state of amnesia with gaps in his memory. Sometime in his past, he was bestowed the knowledge to read the extinct language of '[[Rubix]]'; Carter suspects this is the work of an enigmatic organization known as '[[Scribes|the Scribes]]'.

Carter set out with his friends, the Solar Scion group '[[Soul Shepherds|White Hand]]', under the leadership of [[Animus Tenebris]] to repel the [[Fomorians]] wrecking havoc across the [[Skysea]]. During his travels, the White Hand experienced a devastating event in which everyone but Carter and Animus became suddenly frozen in time. Carter obtained a dangerous artifact known as 'the [[Tome of Time]]' and believes he may have accidentally caused his friends to freeze.

Carter joined [[Characters/SxS Player Characters/Virtus Tenebris]]' [[Soul Shepherds]] shortly after and worked to uncover the mysteries of the Tome of Time and the '[[Rubix Cube|Rubix Cubes]]' scattered across the world.

Carter's family lives on [[Astraloftum]]: his parents [[Kain & Lisandra Moonwing]], his siblings Timmy, Rain, Sera, and his eldest brother [[Simon Moonwing]], the Inquisitor.

## **Lost in Time.** 
The Carter Moonwing that the [[Soul Shepherds]] knew is believed to be trapped in a different [[Timescape]]. When the Time Loop on [[Saturn]] was ended at the [[Elephant Spire]], a temporal swap occurred. The Carter that replaced him came from an alternate [[World-Lines|World Line]] and privately revealed this truth to [[Silas Clemens]], stating he needed Silas and [[Jaz'Farrah]]'s help to return to his own timestream. He and Silas had agreed to help each other in secret.

### **Apostasy & Betrayal.** 
At the Trial of Jaz'Farrah in [[Janus City]], this Carter and Silas enacted a plan: Silas conjured a magical darkness and tore open a spatial rift using a Power Word: Peel scroll, enabling Jaz'Farrah and [[Talleah Preshons]] to escape. Carter fled the courtroom alongside Jaz'Farrah and vanished into the sky. He is now classified as an [[Apostate]] by the [[Ecclesia Solari]]. The [[SABER]] agent [[Thirteen]] has been tasked with hunting him down.

## **Key Tensions.**
- Was possessed by [[Magi]] during the events of the Time Loop and, under divine compulsion, drove a javelin through [[Silas Clemens|Silas]]'s heart. He later used the Magi Tablet to restore Silas's lost arm — an act of atonement or pragmatism, depending on who you ask.
- Discovered evidence via P-Sowen that [[Vincent of Damocles]] has been spying on the Soul Shepherds through a Sol-Phone-like device hidden in P-Sowen's head.
- Knows that the [[Rubix Engine]] is powered by condensed Soul Gems — souls neither alive nor dead, used as harvested energy.
- Is aware that consuming [[Regalo Fruit]] fruit prevents a person from being affected by a Time Loop. Changed his gender when they consumed it.

## Personal Quests.

**Secrets of Rubix** — the Rubix Engine, the Elephant Spire, and the scattered Cubes are all connected.
**Return to the Correct Timestream** — the alternate Carter's primary, secret goal.
