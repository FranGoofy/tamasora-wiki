# Malon

*Spirit Woodcarver — [[Spiritcarving|Spiritcarver]] of the [[Solari Abbey]]*

**Malon** is a young **Spirit** who inhabits a secluded cloister of the [[Solari Abbey]] in [[Astraloftum]]. She appears as a living person at first glance — quiet, talented, and deeply passionate about her craft — but [[Diallos]] eventually learned that she is not mortal at all: she is a Spirit who has chosen to remain in the world, tied to the art she loves.

## Role
Malon introduced [[Diallos]] to the ancient art of **[[Spiritcarving]]**, a rare discipline that blends woodworking, prayer, and soul magic to create **Totems** from wood native to each [[Titan]]. She gave him a **journal cataloging rare woods from every Titan** — effectively sending him on a lifelong quest to carve a complete set of Spirit Totems.

She is gentle, curious, and genuinely delighted by Diallos's progress as a student. Upon his return from [[Saturn]], she marveled at his completed **Totem of Janus** and expressed her own wish to carve one, promising to have one ready for his next return.

## Nature as a Spirit
The revelation that Malon is a Spirit — rather than a living person — raises interesting questions. Why has she remained? What is she attached to? She has shown no signs of malice or sorrow, suggesting her presence in the cloister is one of choice and purpose rather than unresolved grief.

## Connection to the Soul Shepherds
Malon's Spiritcarving quest gives [[Diallos]] a personal and spiritual mission running parallel to the main adventure: to visit every Titan and carve a Totem from its native wood. This ties her art directly to the Soul Shepherds' journey toward [[Paradise]].
