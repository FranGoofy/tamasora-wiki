The self-proclaimed ‘Dread Lord of the [[Great Natu Tree]]’. He is the sole survivor of his family from the blight within the tree and uses necromancy learned from [[Orbs of True Knowledge]] to reanimate the dead to fight the corruption that was once within the tree itself. He is now part of the [[Whispering Grove]] and serves as the Resident Wizard of the Grove.
