# Evil Carter / Carter Moonwing (Alternate Timestream)

*Apostate Solar Scion — Alternate Timestream Carter*

**Evil Carter** (as referred to in the Obsidian index) is a version of [[Carter Moonwing]] from a different timestream who was swapped into the current reality when the **Time Loop** on [[Saturn]] was broken at the [[Elephant Spire]]. The original Carter is trapped in an alternate timestream.

## The Swap
When the 14th Time Loop was broken, a **temporal swap** occurred. The Carter who now walks among the Soul Shepherds — or rather, who has now fled with [[Jaz'Farrah]] — is not the Carter who boarded the [[Project A.R.K.]] from [[Astraloftum]]. This was revealed privately to [[Silas Clemens|Silas]] before the trial in [[Janus City]].

This alternate Carter wishes to return to his own timestream and believes he needs both [[Jaz'Farrah]]'s temporal expertise and Silas's help to do so.

## Actions That Distinguish Him
- Drove a javelin through [[Silas Clemens|Silas]]'s heart during the Saturnus Rex fight, under the command of [[Magi]] — an act the original Carter's moral compass would have resisted more.
- Stated flatly that if Magi asked him to kill [[Talleah Preshons|Talleah]] again, he would comply.
- Slashed [[Jaz'Farrah]]'s cheek to collect her "time-shifted" blood for research.
- Helped orchestrate Jaz'Farrah's escape from the Jas-Sharaza trial, betraying the Soul Shepherds.

## Current Status
**At large.** Escaped Janus City with [[Jaz'Farrah]] following the chaos of the trial. Declared an **[[Apostate]]** by the [[Ecclesia Solari]]. [[Thirteen]], as a [[SABER]] operative, has been tasked with hunting him down.

## The Original Carter
The original Carter Moonwing — brilliant, morally earnest, deeply loyal to the Soul Shepherds — is believed to be trapped in the alternate timestream from which this Carter originated. His fate and potential rescue are unresolved threads.
