# Talleah Preshons

*Silas's Sister — Former Ward of [[Jaz'Farrah]]*

**Talleah Preshons** is the younger sister of [[Silas Clemens]] and the daughter of [[Count von Preshons]], the Fomorian General and Silas's vampiric father. Unlike Silas, Talleah was eventually rescued from the Count's household by [[Jaz'Farrah]], who became a surrogate mother figure to her and introduced her to the Maskin cause on [[Saturn]].

## Background
Talleah and Silas share a traumatic upbringing under the cruelty of [[Count von Preshons]]. Silas left, and Talleah was eventually extracted by Jaz'Farrah. She describes Jaz'Farrah as offering her "unconditional love" — something neither Silas nor the Count ever provided. She became deeply loyal to Jaz'Farrah and her cause, even consuming [[Regalo Fruit]] microdoses across multiple time loops to retain her memories.

## Relationship with Silas
Their reunion on Saturn is one of the most emotionally complex moments of the campaign. Talleah is both relieved to see her brother and deeply tired — having lived through the time loop over a dozen times, she is exhausted by repetition. She holds some bitterness toward Silas for abandoning her, but Silas's accusation that she's "replacing one tyrant for another" resonates with her more than she lets on.

In a touching moment, Talleah requested that Silas consume Regalo Fruit to retain memories of their reunion in case the loop repeated — when Silas refused, she ordered the disguised Soul Shepherds (posing as Maskin) to force-feed it to him. The Scions purposely fumbled the order, earning Talleah's admiration for Silas's combat acrobatics.

## The Trial and Imprisonment
Talleah stood by Jaz'Farrah through her trial before the [[Jas-Sharaza]] Council and voluntarily stepped forward to face the same judgment. When [[Silas Clemens|Silas]] and [[Carter Moonwing|Carter]] enacted their escape plan, Talleah was implicated in the betrayal. She and Silas were arrested under suspicion of treason and transported back to [[Astraloftum]] aboard the [[Project A.R.K.|Project A.R.K.]] as prisoners.

## Current Status
**Prisoner aboard the Project A.R.K.**, transported to Astraloftum for judgment by the [[Ecclesia Solari]].

## Personal Note
*"She saved me from Father's clutches. You abandoned me, and I abandoned you, but Jaz'Farrah gave me hope and unconditional love, something I never received from anyone back home. She understands the feeling of being trapped, and she wants to break everyone free from it."* — Talleah, to Silas
