# Lady Mussurana

**The Naga Emissary of the Spring Court**

Lady Mussurana is a loyal and cunning emissary of the Spring Court, sent by her queen, [[Leira]], on a crucial mission to [[Janus City]].

## Appearance & Nature
- **Naga**: She is a Naga, not a Medusa. Her veiled hair conceals celestial snakes.
- **Personality**: A loyal servant who loves romance novels and is mischievously looking for a husband.

## Mission
- **Objectives**: Monitor the activity of the Ascended [[Solar Scions|Scions]], gather intelligence on the political climate of the [[Titan|Titans]], and obtain magical relics.
- **Interest in Finley**: She has a personal and professional interest in [[Finley]] (the "Prince of Ash"), tasked with keeping tabs on him and reporting back to her queen about his power and connections.
