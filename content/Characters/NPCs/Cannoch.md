Purple [[Tiefling]], a charismatic and loving individual in a relationship with his first mate [[Fern]], was deeply respected by his crew and is considered a threat to the [[Mercantile Hegemony]].

As Ex-[[Golden Navy]], he had mutinied along with Fern after the [[Battle of Ironworks]]. Cannoch now worships [[Natu]] after the [[Soul Shepherds]] helped him turn back to his mortal form from a vegetative state.
