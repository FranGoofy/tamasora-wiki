The Elven Demigod and first-born son of [[Natu]]. Amenó once walked the lands of [[Tamasora]] with [[Fey]] creatures who served the high elves in the **[[Great Wode]]** covering the Titans [[Venus]] and [[Janus City|Janus]].

After a great calamity befell [[Tamasora]] — the details of which Elves refused to share with the rest of the Fey — Amenó left with his closest servants to their own realm and formed the [[Court of Arcadia]] and the [[Gardens of Arcadia]]. He swore never to return to the sky world.

**\[Secret Lore\]** Amenó secretly created a 'Lifebook' containing the entirety of the [[Gardens of Arcadia]], overseen by 'the Librarian', a celestial entity imprisoned by the Gods and enigmatic leader of the [[Scribes]].

Amenó was the first King of the Gardens and the founder of the Court. His offspring all separated into different factions and created the individual sub courts: The [[Court of Spring]] and Orchid Courts are one of the same, and then the others — Autumn, Summer, Winter.
