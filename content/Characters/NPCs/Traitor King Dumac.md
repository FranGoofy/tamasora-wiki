The current illegitimate ruler of [[Jupiter]], son of [[Editor Rupert]] and [[King Dumaclath]]. He sold out the dwarven kingdom of [[Jupiter|Carapax]] to Admiral [[Cloudbeard]] to save his throne and has enslaved the [[Shellkin Nation]] and dissenting dwarves in toxic forge-factories to refine Martian [[Orichalcum]] and mine [[Adamantine]]. 

**Abilities and Equipment.** He hides behind a massive mechanical exoskeleton commissioned to protect him - both physically and mentally.
