A Giant pirate member of the [[Cloudbeard Pirates]] known as "Runemaiden". The Stone [[Giant]] Corsair from whom the [[Soul Shepherds]] rescued Joy. She was Polymorphed into a Turtle by [[Milodious Aureon]] and survived the battle with the Scions - she has returned to Jupiter to inform [[Cloudbeard]] of the Scions’ capabilities.
