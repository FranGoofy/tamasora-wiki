*Roaming Spider Merchant of the [[Skysea]]

Encountered by the rookies in Spider City during the [[Battle of Spider Isle]]. Tipped them off that [[Animus Tenebris|Animus]] had gone into the Palace to face the [[Spider Queen]]. Later advised them on bringing flower offerings for the new queens.

## On Jupiter (Grand Aletop)
Jingma has surfaced again at the [[Grand Aletop]] in [[Hipolum-Dhrum]], following the smuggler-roads down into the [[Undershell]]. She remembers the Soul Shepherds, and [[Whimsical|Whimsy]]'s Spider Badges in particular. Her new stock is grimly well-timed for a party about to descend into a burning factory full of giants.

### New Wares
- **Cindersilk Shawl.** Spidersilk cured in fire-country dye. Advantage on saves against the [[The Old Palace|Old Palace]]'s High Heat, and once per day, resistance to fire damage until the end of your next turn.
- **Gossamer Line (50 ft).** A spool of living spidersilk that climbs or anchors on command: a self-belaying rope for the giant-scale winches and shafts.
- **Clingsmoke Vials (x3).** Throwable cocoons of grey web-smoke that burst into a 10-foot cloud of obscuring strands (heavily obscured, difficult terrain). Good for an exit.
- **Whisperstep Wraps.** Muffling silk foot-wraps: advantage on Stealth checks made to move quietly.
- **Morphic Veil.** A gauzy half-mask of shifting silk that helps a disguise hold under scrutiny (advantage to maintain a Disguise Self / Seeming / shapeshift when someone looks too closely). A quiet gift for a certain "dwarf" who keeps slipping out of his shape, or for [[Milodious Aureon|Milo]] and [[Whimsical|Whimsy]].
