A veteran Dwarven leader of the [[Scribes]], historian, and [[Joy]]’s closest confidante. Rupert harbored a massive secret: he and the previous [[King Dumaclath]] were once lovers. They had a fundamental disagreement that caused Rupert to leave [[Jupiter]] long ago. It turns out the current usurper, [[Traitor King Dumac]], is actually Rupert’s son. Rupert is now committed to helping bring down his own flesh and blood.
