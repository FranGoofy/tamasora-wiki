*Hawk companion of [[Diallos]]*

Eden is the loyal hawk bonded to [[Diallos]]. She acts as his scout, messenger, and occasional helper in both combat and quieter moments — sharp-eyed enough to spot threats and treasures the rest of the [[Soul Shepherds]] miss.

## Notable Moments
- [[Battle at the Well of Souls]](Session 22).** During the battle against [[Count von Preshons]], Diallos sent Eden upwards into the canopy of the [[Great Natu Tree]] to call on the Guardian of Love's Fragment for aid.
- **Saturnus Rex Cave ([[Saturn]] arc).** Discovered a teleport scroll inside the lair of the [[Saturnus Rex]] — Diallos used it to extract the [[Soul Shepherds]] (with an unconscious [[Silas Clemens|Silas]] in tow) before the cave collapsed.
- **[[Quadrennial Meal Festival]], Round 2.** Spotted [[Helga the Ogre Baker]] accidentally walking off with the team's Aquitania Pearl Oyster from the pantry, allowing Diallos to retrieve the key ingredient before the cook-off started.
