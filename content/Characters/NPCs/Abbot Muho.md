The eccentric and somewhat womanizing abbot of the [[Solari Abbey]]. He is a respected spiritual leader who tutored [[Diallos]]. He agreed to train [[Animus Tenebris]] to bring order to his mind and emotions and put Diallos in charge of training [[Silas Clemens]] to exorcize the [[Souls]] from his [[Phylactery]] body. He also traveled to the battlefield to give last rites to the fallen soldiers of the [[Fomorian Invasion of Astraloftum]]. He’s gone on a spiritual mission (astral projection) to hunt something or someone down…
