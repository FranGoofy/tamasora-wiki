An Arch-[[Demon]] that [[Thirteen]] fought during a mission gone wrong. Grythe is the demon whose death — at Thirteen's hands — came at the cost of Thirteen's entire squad, whose [[Souls]] Thirteen accidentally consumed in the process. This event led to Thirteen's trial before the [[Solari Inquisition]] and his eventual induction into [[SABER]].

*"Where is the little light?"* — Grythe, searching for the invisible, grief-stricken Thirteen on the empty battlefield.
