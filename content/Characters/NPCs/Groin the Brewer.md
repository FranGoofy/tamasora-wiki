*Dwarven Barkeep — [[Inverted Temple]] Alchemical Library*

**Groin the Brewer** is a grizzled, cantankerous Dwarven Brewer who operates out of the Alchemical Library of the [[Inverted Temple]], the [[Bhan-Ember]] and [[Scribes]] resistance base in [[Jupiter]]'s [[Undershell]]. He is the Alchemical Library's self-appointed guardian and the last living keeper of the Ambrosius brewing traditions.

## Personality
Gruff, curmudgeonly, and intensely passionate about ale — specifically about finding the lost recipe for **Bugman's Ale**. He does not suffer fools and is quick to accuse strangers of being [[Ecclesia Solari|Solari]] spies.

*"Don't blow the froth off! It's the best part!"*

## What He Knows
- The Ambrosius Monks once meditated in the temple on the socialities of their lives.
- [[Saint Brasser the Ambrosius|Brasser]] lost his mind after staring too long into the waters of **[[Lake Ruthaba]]**.
- He is desperately seeking the lost recipe for **Bugman's Ale** — a legendary brew of extraordinary potency that bolsters resolve and grants temporary immunity to fear. Despite the destruction of the brewery, the creator **Josef Bugman** survived, and the ale can still be found in famous taverns.

## Hidden Knowledge *(Revealed on Good Roleplay / High Insight)*
He can point out a **loose rock carving** that conceals a lost Dwarven distilling technique. The ale produced from this technique grants immunity to fear for several hours — though it counts as four drinks due to its potency.

## Negative Reaction *(Bad Roleplay / Failed Deception)*
He loudly accuses the party of being [[Ecclesia Solari]] spies sent to "finish the de-canonization job" on Brasser.
