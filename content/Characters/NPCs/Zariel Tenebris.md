# Zariel Tenebris

*Lord Commander of [[Astraloftum]] — Mother of [[Virtus Tenebris|Virtus]] and [[Animus Tenebris|Animus]]*

**Zariel Tenebris** is the Lord Commander of Astraloftum and one of its most formidable defenders. The widow (or rather, the abandoned partner) of the missing [[Amiel Tenebris]], Zariel has spent the years since his disappearance holding the city together at great personal cost.

## Personality
A woman of iron discipline and suppressed grief. Zariel masks her pain behind the armor of the Lord Commander — a role she performs with genuine dedication. She can be cold and commanding, but beneath that lies a mother who is deeply lonely and terrified of being the only one left holding the Tenebris legacy.

## Relationship with Vincent
After Amiel's disappearance, Zariel entered a relationship with [[Vincent of Damocles]], an old "friend" of her husband. She allowed Vincent to repurpose the [[Tenebris Estate]] into a Solari Crusader training academy, convinced by his logic that the estate had become "a museum dedicated to what we lost."

She was unaware until [[Virtus Tenebris|Virtus]] confronted her that Vincent had auctioned off the **[[J.T. Holy Chest|J.T. Holy Mahogany Chest]]** — Sir Amiel's personal heirloom — to pirates at the [[Scheppihn Auction House]] in Janus City.

## Turn Against Vincent
Armed with the proof the Soul Shepherds uncovered from Vincent's study — including a **Death Note** listing people marked as "Harvested" or "Pending," with Zariel herself marked "H" — Zariel has begun formulating a plan to excise Vincent from the Tenebris legacy. She summoned him to the Cathedral under a false audit pretense to allow the Soul Shepherds to infiltrate his private study.

She declared: *"No stranger will auction off our blood for gold."* She has handed [[Virtus Tenebris|Virtus]] the **Tenebris Master Key**.

## Role in the Fomorian Invasion
Zariel fought on the front lines of the [[Fomorian Invasion of Astraloftum]], leading the coalition army of Inquisition soldiers and Solar Scions alongside [[Animus Tenebris|Animus]] and [[High Priestess Cetra|Liara Cetra]]. She was gravely wounded by a Chimeron during the battle when Animus was momentarily distracted.

## Current Status
Active in Astraloftum. Allied with the Soul Shepherds against Vincent. Planning to reclaim the Tenebris legacy.
