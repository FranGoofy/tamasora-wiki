A [[Tiefling]] priestess and the head of [[Natu's Starlit Nest]], [[Astraloftum]]'s orphanage within the [[Solari Abbey]]. She is the foster mother of [[Silas Clemens]] and one of the few people who can read [[Rubix]], the ancient language of the ancients.

She rescued Silas from [[Count von Preshons]] during [[The Fall of Yahar’gul]].
