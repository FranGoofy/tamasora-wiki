*Missing Solar Scion — Father of [[Virtus Tenebris|Virtus]] and [[Animus Tenebris|Animus]]*

**Sir Amiel Tenebris the Maulforged** was a legendary [[Solar Scions|Solar Scion]], renowned warrior, and patriarch of the [[Tenebris Estate]]. He disappeared approximately ten years ago during an expedition to find [[Paradise]], leaving behind his family, his legacy, and many unanswered questions.

## Legacy
Amiel was celebrated as one of the most skilled Solar Scions of his generation. The Tenebris name is synonymous with martial excellence and devotion to [[Tekk]]. His disappearance was the event that set both [[Virtus Tenebris|Virtus]] and [[Animus Tenebris|Animus]] on the path to becoming Solar Scions themselves.

The **Shrine of Tekk** at the Tenebris Estate was maintained in his honor, though it fell into disrepair after his disappearance.

## Personal Effects
- **The [[J.T. Holy Chest|J.T. Holy Mahogany Chest]]** — A sacred heirloom that [[Vincent of Damocles|Vincent]] auctioned off during the estate sale. It ended up in Janus City, which raises questions about how it traveled so far.
- **His study and personal belongings** were left in the estate before it was converted into a training academy by Vincent.

## Connections
- **[[Vincent of Damocles]]** - Claimed to be Amiel's "old friend." His true relationship to Amiel - and whether he knows more about the disappearance than he admits - is deeply suspicious.
- **[[Animus Tenebris|Animus]]** - Spoke of Amiel having "a close friend on Aphra," an elusive Aphrazonian who might hold clues to his whereabouts. Aphra's rule that only women may enter complicates access.
- **[[Zariel Tenebris|Zariel]]** - His partner; has been holding the family together since his disappearance, at great personal cost.
- [[Bobby Beerbelly]] - His lifelong friend and fellow Scion. They fought together during the [[Battle of the Shield]]. He disappeared alongside Amiel 10 years ago.

## Clues & Mysteries
- Vincent told [[Silas Clemens|Silas]] that a **clue to Paradise is hidden inside the [[Project A.R.K.|airship]]** — this was given without context but may relate to Amiel's last expedition notes.
- The J.T. Chest appearing in Janus City suggests Amiel's last journey may have taken him to [[Saturn]] or that the chest passed through many hands after his disappearance.
- **His fate is unknown.** Rumors persist but no body or definitive evidence of death has surfaced.

## Current Status
**Missing.** His fate is one of the Soul Shepherds' central personal mysteries.
