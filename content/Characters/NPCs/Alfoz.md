# Alfoz

The **Foreman** of the [[Bhan-Ember]] resistance movement on the [[Titan]] [[Jupiter]]. Alfoz oversees construction of the towering banner of [[Tekk]] in the Bhan-Ember Amphitheatre — a colossal symbol of faith and defiance against [[Traitor King Dumac]] and the [[Cloudbeard Pirates]].

Little else is currently known about Alfoz's background, though their position as Foreman suggests considerable organizational skill and authority within the resistance.
