**(Status Unknown)** The revered previous ruler of Jupiter who kept the deep roads sealed to protect the [[Titan]] from [[King Zaar]]'s blight. It was believed his son [[Traitor King Dumac]] usurped and murdered him. However, deep rumors suggest King Dumaclath’s grave was taken to the [[Deep Roads]] and his death was faked. He is still worshipped in secret by devoted dwarves.
