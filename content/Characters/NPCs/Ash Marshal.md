The only member of the [[Court of Arcadia]] regularly sent seeking across the planes. Dispatched by [[Amenó]] (Val) to bring those who would harm his creation to justice. He is [[Amenó]]'s [[Timescape|timescape]] hunter and carries much deep lore about the multiverse.

The Marshal calls upon [[Amenó]] to aid him in battle, and [[Amenó]] infuses his hunter with arcane power. His spellcasting relies on his ability to convince [[Amenó]] that the need for aid is great.

**Tactics.** The Marshal identifies the greatest immediate threat to whoever summoned him and attacks with the Ashsword, hoping to censure the target. His spells are almost all offensive, but he always has counterspell ready to thwart enemy spellcasters, and banishment in case the enemy has summoned members of the Court of All Flesh.
