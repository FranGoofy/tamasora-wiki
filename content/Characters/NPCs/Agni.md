A middle-aged [[Elf]] and the only one on Astraloftum. He is an [[Archdruid]] respected for his gentle demeanor and exceptional healing skills, serving as the primary caretaker of the injured creatures and plants in the [[Whispering Grove]]. He has piercing red eyes, hair the color of autumn leaves, and wears a cloak of woven vines. He is the father of [[Dahlia]] and [[Erevan]]. His soul was consumed by a demon, leaving him an undead husk, but [[Holy Mother Anastasia]] cast 'Gentle Repose' on him and is now recovering on [[Astraloftum]].
