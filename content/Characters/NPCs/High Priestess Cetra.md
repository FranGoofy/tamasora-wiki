High Priestess of the [[Ecclesia Solari]] and leader of the [[Astraloftum]] Church Hierarchy. Under her leadership, the Church remains steadfast in its mission to locate [[Paradise]], believing the arrival of the [[Comet of Eden]] marks the beginning of a new crusade.

She resides in [[St. Ode'bbej Cathedral]].

The elusive ‘blind’ High Priestess of the [[Ecclesia Solari]] (who has forsaken her name) and also the much more public facing [[Solar Scions]] Liara Cetra. Few know about her double persona. 

Liara's ultimate dream is to have a temple devoted to the [[Trinity]] in every city and village. She is a bit of a troublemaker that mostly focuses on teaching new rookies the ways of the Scions, Liara is also an emissary to 'the Elder’, a Solar Dragon named **Luminaria** that ascends Solari Rookies into Solar Scions. Liara’s devotion to the Trinity leads her to be intolerant of other faiths. She believes that the Three represents civilization's best chance to survive in a world that is filled with monsters, evil and ugliness. She leads the secret organization SABER and is the surrogate mother of **Thirteen**.

She can hear conversations remotely, seemingly anywhere on Astraloftum and possibly extending into the Skysea itself. This ability can be circumvented by communicating nonverbally such as writing on a piece of paper.
