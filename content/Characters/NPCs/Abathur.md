# Abathur

*Eldritch Deity — Corruptor of [[Lake Ruthaba]]*

**Abathur** is an eldritch deity whose influence corrupts the waters of **[[Lake Ruthaba]]** deep in the [[Undershell]] of [[Jupiter]]. 

**GM Note:** An unlikely ally to the [[Bhan-Ember]] resistance and the [[Soul Shepherds]]?

## Nature
Abathur is not a member of the [[Trinity]] — it is something older and more alien, an entity whose power seeps through the deep stone of [[Carapax]]. Its influence manifests through the corrupted waters of Lake Ruthaba, which mutate creatures that drink from them and whisper to those who rest nearby.

A **shrine to Abathur** exists beneath the surface of Lake Ruthaba, accessible only by diving into the murky, mutated depths. The **[[Golden Key]]** needed to open the **Door to the Eternal Keg** in the [[Inverted Temple]] rests at this shrine.

## The Corruption of Brasser
**[[Saint Brasser the Ambrosius]]** did not lose his faith — he was deliberately exposed to Abathur's corruption by a **high-ranking [[Ecclesia Solari|Solari]] official** who wanted the Undershell sealed forever. The Church's official account, that Brasser "went mad from weak faith," is a sanctioned lie. The truth is recorded in a heretical manuscript hidden in the binding of a book within the [[Inverted Temple]]'s Alchemical Library.

[[Thirteen]]'s Soul-Sword reacts violently near the area where this manuscript is hidden, drawn by the presence of corrupted soul-energy.

## Previous Interactions with the Scions
**Bobby Beerbelly** — brother of Saint Brasser and a [[Solar Scions|Solar Scion]] who traveled with [[Amiel Tenebris|Sir Amiel]] — discovered the truth and recorded it in his personal journals, also stored in the Alchemical Library. He learned that Brasser's madness came from Abathur's corruption, not spiritual weakness.

Bobby is believed to have possessed the **Golden Key** before his disappearance alongside Amiel. A blood-stained journal entry from Amiel reads: *"The Fomorian is dead, but the Keg is breached. Bobby has the Golden Key. I will hold the line at the bridge so he can seal the door. Tell Virtus I fought in the light."*

## Connection to the Golden Key
The Golden Key — seen in visions by both [[Thirteen]] and [[Bartholomew Reginald Baldwin XXIX|Bartholomew]] — rests at Abathur's shrine beneath Lake Ruthaba. Retrieving it requires diving into the mutated waters and interacting with whatever lies at the shrine's depths.

**Long Ago**. Abathur once lived in the [[The Lost Sea Beneath the Clouds]], and faced off against [[Khemmis]] and the [[Weastern Pirates]]. 

## History
**Abathur** is an ancient [[Olothec]] called by the slime servants who worship them as a living god, stole forth from [[The Lost Sea Beneath the Clouds]] centuries ago to conquer humanoid lands. The **Olothec** followed the courses of lakes and rivers across a half-dozen realms, leaving death and mayhem in their wake. But upon escaping the [[Titan God War]] by hiding in [[Jupiter]]'s [[Undershell]], he found a war-shattered lakeside ruin ([[Lake Ruthaba]], that it now calls 'Home'.

Over the decades since, **Abathur** has been breeding an army of slime servants in the flooded **Deep Roads**, overseeing countless Dwarven Kings and Queens reign over the centuries; waiting - preparing for a day when their army will spill forth to seize the Titan for itself. 

Explorers who slip past the **slime servant patrols** must navigate the collapsing tunnels and passageways that fill the Lake's caverns. These treacherous tunnels are all coated with thick slime, allowing **Abathur** to use their swimming speed to hunt intruders in their lair.

**Abathur** has an army of jellyfied [[Dreamers]] (Ooze Kuotoa) that they found in the [[Dreamcaves|Dreamcave]] below Ruthaba and now serve as their thrall.
