# Saint Ascon

*"He served the Trinity longer than any of his living students were alive. When the bell finally tolled for him, it tolled for a man none of them had ever truly known."*

**Saint Ascon Vaylin Solari** was the former High Priest of the [[Ecclesia Solari]], the immediate predecessor of [[High Priestess Cetra]], and the longest-reigning head of the Church in over four centuries. To the public, he was a paragon: a wartime saint canonized in his own lifetime, the architect of the Church's most secret order, the gentle voice behind a generation of doctrine. To those now sifting through the rooms he left behind, he was the rot at the center of the white walls.

The full extent of Ascon's corruption is the central political project of [[High Priestess Cetra|Cetra]]'s reign. Every month, more of it is uncovered. Every month, fewer of the people who could speak to it remain alive.

## Early Life & Rise

Ascon was born in a parish chapel in one of the lower districts of [[Astraloftum]] (the Aeritha District, by some accounts; the Sun Docks, by others). His parents were lay devotees, neither rich nor connected. He was sent to the [[Solari Abbey]] as a child, where he was taught alongside another orphan who would later become **[[Abbot Muho]]**. The two were close as brothers; surviving instructors of that generation describe them as inseparable until they were almost grown.

Ascon distinguished himself early as a scholar of the **[[Solari Sermons]]**, with a particular gift for the contradictions between the three gods. By his twenties he was an acknowledged authority on the *Sermon of Fomorians*; by his thirties he was sitting on the Solar Conclave as the youngest High Cardinal of [[Magi]] in living memory. He was raised to High Priest at the age of forty-one.

He held the office for **forty-seven years**. He was eighty-eight when he died.

## Canonization

Most Saints are canonized after their deaths, often centuries later. Ascon was canonized at **sixty-three**, twenty-two years into his reign, while still alive and presiding over the Conclave that elevated him. The grounds were the **Miracle at the Cinder Gate**: a Fomorian incursion struck the southern docks of [[Astraloftum]] during the month of [[Cindertide]], and Ascon (according to the public record) walked unarmed and unarmored into the breach, raised one hand, and turned the front line of demons to ash with a single recitation of the Edicts of Balance. Hundreds of witnesses swore to it. The Trinity granted the canonization unanimously.

A handful of survivors of the Cinder Gate, now very old or very dead, have privately told a different story. Some say the demons were already turning back when Ascon arrived. Others say he was not alone. One, before his death, told [[Abbot Muho]] that there had been a *second* unmasked figure on the front line that day, and that the figure walked away wearing Ascon's robes when it was done. Muho has never publicly repeated this.

The canonization was the largest single grant of papal authority in the modern Ecclesia. It is also, in Cetra's view, the keystone Ascon used to place himself above doctrinal review.

## The Alleged Corruptions

[[High Priestess Cetra|Cetra]]'s ongoing investigation has confirmed, suggested, or implicated Ascon in the following acts. The list is not exhaustive.

### The Poisoning of Saint Brasser

Ascon is now believed to be the senior Solari official who orchestrated the deliberate exposure of **[[Saint Brasser the Ambrosius]]** to the corruption of **[[Abathur]]**. His motive was to seal the [[Undershell]] of [[Jupiter]] forever, severing the Ecclesia's connection to the Dwarven faith and removing a parallel Tekk-cult he viewed as a doctrinal threat. He then orchestrated Brasser's **de-canonization** to bury the truth.

The half-burned name in the heretical manuscript hidden in the [[Inverted Temple]]'s Alchemical Library is widely believed by those in the know to be Ascon's. The other name, [[Vincent of Damocles|Vincent of Damocles']] ancestor **Vincent the Elder**, is fully legible. Ascon's, true to form, is the one obscured.

### The Sealed Investigations

Several major Church inquiries were quietly closed under Ascon's signature, with the records sealed under his personal authority:

- **The [[Ruby Incident]]:** The destruction of [[Redhome]] by Apostate Scion [[Gragun Tides]] thirty-five years ago. The official reason for the seal is "ongoing operational concern." The actual reason is unknown, but the seal has never been lifted, and Cetra is currently weighing whether to break it.
- The disappearance of three Solar Scion expeditions that approached the **Frozen Shell** of [[Jupiter]] in the years before [[Cloudbeard]]'s rise.
- The original investigation into the foundering of the **[[Battle of the Shield]]**, in which Ascon authorized the Church's involvement in supplying Soul-Bound [[Automaton|Automatons]] to the [[Mercantile Hegemony]] and the [[Martian Imperium]]. The Church's complicity has never been publicly acknowledged.

### The Founding of [[SABER]]

After [[Amiel Tenebris]]'s disappearance, Ascon discovered (or claimed to discover) the **Scriptures of False Rites**: desecrated scripture allegedly from [[Paradise]] itself, written by what Ascon named the **[[Cult of Nirvana]]**. The Scriptures named corrupted Scions, Apostates who had become demonic infiltrators within the Ecclesia, falsifying miracles for political gain.

Ascon's response was to found **[[SABER]]**, the silent counter-knife of the Church. He devised the **[[Light Severance]]** rite, the **[[Twilight Brand]]**, and the protocols by which Sci-Ops live as if excommunicated. The first generation of operatives reported only to him.

Cetra inherited SABER on Ascon's death. She has spent the years since auditing every operation he ever authorized. Several of his named "Apostates" have, on review, turned out to be political rivals. At least three were innocent. Two of those three are still missing.

### The Litany Erasures

The **[[Ecclesia Solari#The Litany of Names|Litany of Names]]** is the binding annual recitation of every excommunicated Soul. Under Ascon, the Litany was twice silently shortened. Names were removed without trial, hearing, or successor's approval. The two interventions are, in Cetra's eyes, the most damning evidence of his abuse of office, because the only reason to *remove* a name from the Litany is to permit a particular Apostate to return wearing a different face. **Whose names** Ascon removed is the secret Cetra most wants to know, and the secret most carefully buried.

## Relationship with [[Abbot Muho]]

Ascon and **[[Abbot Muho]]** were raised together in the [[Solari Abbey]]. They were, by every surviving account, inseparable for the first thirty years of their lives. They were each other's first Sun-Sworn (see **[[Ecclesia Solari#The Sun-Sworn Vow|the Sun-Sworn Vow]]**), the names they whispered to the rising sun for two decades.

Their split came in the year **278**, during what is now called **the Long Argument**: a sequence of three private debates held over the course of one autumn within the walls of the Abbey. No record of what was actually said has ever surfaced. What is known:

- They went in friends.
- They came out enemies.
- Within the year, Muho had renounced his cardinalship and withdrawn into the contemplative orders, never to hold formal political authority again.
- Within five years, Ascon had been canonized.

The two never spoke directly again. They were in the same room only once more, the day Muho came to deliver the last rites to Ascon's body. Witnesses say Muho stood at the bedside in silence for several minutes, then bowed once and left without speaking the words. The rites were given by a Cardinal of [[Magi]] instead. Muho has never explained.

It is widely (and quietly) suspected that what Muho saw, or did not see, in that room is part of the reason he has now departed [[Astraloftum]] on his current astral projection: he is **hunting something** that the official record says died forty-three years ago.

## The Death of Saint Ascon

*"He was the most public man in the city for almost half a century. He died in the only room nobody was looking at."*

The official account, as recorded in the **Book of Passings** of [[St. Ode'bbej Cathedral]], states that Saint Ascon **died peacefully in his sleep** on the night of the **17th of [[Ashfall]], Year 268**, attended by his confessor and discovered by the Cathedral's morning bell-ringer. His funeral was held three days later. His body lay in state for the city to file past, and was interred in the **Crypt of High Priests** beneath the Cathedral.

Almost nothing in that account is verifiable.

- The confessor named in the Book of Passings was [[Abbot Muho|Muho]]. Muho was not in [[Astraloftum]] that night. He was at the Solari Abbey, in the company of three other monks, all of whom have testified to his presence under oath. Muho has never offered to correct the record.
- The bell-ringer who allegedly discovered the body left the Church the following month. She was never seen in [[Astraloftum]] again. Her current whereabouts are unknown.
- The body that lay in state was viewed by tens of thousands of mourners. At least two High Cardinals present (now both deceased) privately reported that the corpse's hands did not bear the **Sun-Crown Brand**, the ceremonial mark every High Priest receives at elevation. The body's face was, by all accounts, Ascon's. The hands were not.
- The **Crypt of High Priests** has, on the rare occasions Cetra has authorized a check, been found to contain Ascon's casket exactly where it was placed. The casket is **empty**, and has been since at least the year 290. No record exists of when, or by whom, the body was removed. The seal on the casket was not broken.

The cause of death is, officially, "natural." The actual cause is unknown. The actual location of the body is unknown. The actual *date* of death, if Ascon died at all, is unknown.

## Where His Soul Resides

Saint Ascon was the first High Priest in the modern Ecclesia to formally vow that his Soul, upon his passing, would be **shepherded into the [[Great Natu Tree]]** to await the restoration of [[Paradise]]. The Symphony of Ascension was performed for him with full Conclave honors during the funeral. The Archdruids of the [[Whispering Grove]] confirmed the Soul's reception. The matter was considered closed.

It is now considered, very privately, considerably less closed.

Since the **[[Fomorian Invasion of Astraloftum]]**, the souls within the [[Great Natu Tree]] have been placed into **Temporal Stasis** and cannot be communed with. This was true even before the Invasion for one specific Soul: **Ascon's**. From the day his Soul was supposedly received, the Archdruids who attempted to commune with him reported only **silence**. Not absence. Silence. *"Like a room with the door closed,"* one wrote, *"and someone standing behind it on the other side, also not speaking."*

Three possibilities are debated by the very small number of people who know about the silence:

1. **He is there.** His Soul rests in the Tree, but for reasons of his own design will not speak. This is the official position when the matter is forced.
2. **He is not there.** His Soul was never delivered. The Symphony of Ascension was either faked, intercepted, or shepherded the wrong Soul into the Tree. Where his Soul actually went is anyone's guess.
3. **He is there, and he is awake.** This is the possibility no one will say out loud. The Souls within the Tree are not supposed to retain individuality past a certain depth. If Ascon's has, and if his is the *only* one that has, then the Tree contains something the Church has not yet acknowledged it contains.

[[High Priestess Cetra|Cetra]] has not attempted to commune with him. The reason she gives publicly is respect for the dead. The reason she gives privately, to the perhaps three people she trusts, is that she suspects he is waiting for *exactly* that.

## The Saint Who Will Not Be Named at Sunrise

By order of [[High Priestess Cetra|Cetra]], issued in the third year of her reign and never publicly recorded, no living member of the Ecclesia Solari clergy is permitted to take **Saint Ascon's name as their Sun-Sworn**. This is the only such proscription in the Church's history. The Sun-Sworn is supposed to be a free and personal vow; no other Saint, sinner, or stranger has ever been excluded.

The reason given to the clergy is *"out of reverence for his completed work."* The reason itself is older and uglier: Cetra suspects that to name Ascon at sunrise is to give him a way back.

## Open Threads
*(For the Soul Shepherds and the GM)*

- **The half-burned name** in the [[Inverted Temple]] manuscript is, on present evidence, Ascon's. Confirming this would publicly indict the most beloved High Priest in living memory and would require evidence the Church does not want recovered.
- **[[Abbot Muho]]'s astral projection** has been described as a hunt. He has not said what he hunts. Players who reach him in spirit may be the first outsiders to learn.
- **The empty casket.** Whoever moved the body, moved it without breaking the seal. This is theologically impossible by every Church doctrine on holy interments. Someone with the seal's authority did it. The list of suspects is very short.
- **The bell-ringer.** She was last seen leaving [[Astraloftum]] aboard a freighter bound for the [[Mercantile Hegemony]]'s freightyards. If she lives, she is in her seventies now, and she is the only mortal known to have looked at Ascon's corpse without a Church sanction on her shoulder.
- **The Sun-Crown Brand.** Whoever's hands lay in state in 268, those hands were never marked by the rite of elevation. Either Ascon was not in his own coffin, or Ascon was never the High Priest he claimed to be.
