# Jaz'Farrah

*Mad Shamaness of Saturn — Maskin Revolutionary*

**Jaz'Farrah** is a [[Children of Jas|Titanborn]] shamaness from [[Saturn]] who led the **Maskin** faction in a controversial campaign to end the [[Jas-Sharaza]] policy of strict isolation and open the borders of Saturn to the outside world. Her methods — including trafficking sacred [[Regalo Fruit]], assassinating political figures, and accidentally triggering a catastrophic time loop — earned her the title of "Mad Shamaness."

## Background
Jaz'Farrah was prosecuted at a young age for her gifts and was seemingly "destined for madness" by those around her. This persecution shaped her into a deeply traumatized but brilliant individual who believes her radical methods are the only correct path. Over time, her methods grew increasingly devious, culminating in the Jaz'Farrah the Soul Shepherds encountered.

She saved [[Silas Clemens|Silas]]'s sister, [[Talleah Preshons]], from [[Count von Preshons|Count von Preshons']] clutches and became a surrogate mother figure to her. Talleah describes Jaz'Farrah as offering her "unconditional love" and an understanding of what it means to feel trapped.

## The Time Loop
Jaz'Farrah accidentally triggered a [[Rubix Cube]] time loop on Saturn that repeated **14 times** before the Soul Shepherds finally broke the cycle. Her intent was to use the Loop to shut down the **Elephant Spire** — a giant black spear embedded in [[Saturn|Janus's]] back that she believed was causing the Titan's suffering. She also wished to remove the Spire to free the [[Children of Jas]] from their centuries of forced isolation.

## Abilities
- Mastery of temporal energies, rendering her immune to Chronomancy spells due to constant exposure.
- Can reverse time locally (demonstrated when she rewound Silas snatching the Tome of Time).
- Wings of midnight black, capable of flight.
- Commands **Sandy** summoned hourglass fairies.
- Possesses a deep understanding of [[Rubix Cube|Rubix]] manipulation.
- Has the "time-shifted" blood that [[Carter Moonwing|Carter]] collected for research purposes.

## Trial & Escape
Captured by the Soul Shepherds and brought before the [[Jas-Sharaza]] Council at [[Elefanti Palace]], Jaz'Farrah was sentenced to execution. Before the sentence could be carried out, [[Silas Clemens|Silas]] and [[Carter Moonwing|Carter]] enacted a secret plan: Silas unleashed magical darkness and used a scroll of **Power Word: Peel** to tear a spatial rift, allowing Jaz'Farrah and Carter to escape. [[Finley]] knocked Jaz'Farrah into the rift during the chaos; she and Carter subsequently vanished into the sky together.

## Current Status
**At large.** Last seen escaping Janus City with [[Carter Moonwing|Carter]] after the trial. Her whereabouts are unknown. [[Talleah Preshons|Talleah]], who stood by her side through the trial, was arrested alongside [[Silas Clemens|Silas]] in the aftermath and transported back to Astraloftum. She and Carter are looking for the missing pages of the [[Tome of Time]]. 

## Relationships
- **[[Talleah Preshons]]** – Ward and surrogate daughter. Talleah's loyalty was tested but never quite broken.
- **[[Jer'Emee]]** – Former ally turned critic. Jer'Emee ultimately denounced her plan as ignorant of Saturn's deeper history.
- **[[Carter Moonwing]]** – Reluctant collaborator turned escape partner. Carter collected her time-shifted blood and views her as "still useful."
- **[[Silas Clemens]]** – Adversary and estranged brother of her ward. Despite their conflict, Silas's actions saved her from execution.
- [[Milodious Aureon]] - Distant cousin.
