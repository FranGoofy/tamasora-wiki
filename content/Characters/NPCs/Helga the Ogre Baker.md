A friendly [[Fomorians]] [[Ogre]] who once met the [[Solar Scions]] on [[Aluminux]] and who then met them again on [[Saturn]] ([[Janus]]), she participated in the [[Quadrennial Meal Festival]]. She befriended a small dinosaur as her sous chef, which she later served as a dish. She has taken a liking to [[Silas Clemens]].
