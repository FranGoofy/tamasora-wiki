# Maverick

*Windmill Lift Operator — [[Janus City]] Gatekeeper*

**Maverick** is an old, feathered man who operates the **windmill lift** that serves as one of the access routes up into [[Janus City]] on [[Saturn]]. He has a faithful flying dog named **Margarine** who accompanies him.

## Encounter with the Soul Shepherds
When the Soul Shepherds arrived at the steep outcrop where the windmill lift is located, Jer'Emee called out for Maverick — with no response. [[Silas Clemens|Silas]] climbed up and found only Margarine and an empty windmill.

The mystery was solved when [[Diallos]] noticed a rope tied to the whirling windmill blades — **Maverick was bound to the blades**, fast asleep, having been tied there by hooligans who didn't want him letting foreigners into the city. [[Carter Moonwing|Carter]] halted the gears with Chronurgy magic, allowing Diallos to catch Maverick as he dropped. Margarine revived him with enthusiastic dog kisses.

## Information He Provided
- He informed the Scions that **14 days** had passed in the outside world during their time in the loops (confirmed by a newspaper).
- An airship from [[Astraloftum]] had recently arrived in Janus City with goods for the upcoming [[Quadrennial Meal Festival]] Auction and left just as quickly.
- He suggested **[[Freedom's Respite]]** as the best inn for travelers.
- He offered the Scions an unconventional route into Janus City — an **unstable mine cart track** that shot them screaming through the mountain, requiring a last-moment leap aided by [[Characters/SxS Player Characters/Virtus Tenebris|Virtus]]'s Pegasus Feather.
