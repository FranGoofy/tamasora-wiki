An eccentric Panda inventor and [[Titanborn]] shipwright who owns "[[Beppo Co. Aeroworks]]" in [[Janus City]]. He was enlisted by the [[Soul Shepherds]] to repair their [[Zephyr]] the [[A.R.K]]. 

He refuses to work with [[Sky Pirates]] and is building a water pump prototype due to a [[Prophecy]]from his deceased daughter. He is now traveling with the Soul Shepherds on a quest to deliver his daughter’s Soul (which he keeps in a tool he made) to [[Paradise]]. 

While the Soul Shepherds explore [[Jupiter]], Beppo and the automaton [[P-Sowen]] have been left temporarily in charge of the Project A.R.K.
