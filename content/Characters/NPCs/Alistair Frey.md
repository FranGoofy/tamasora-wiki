A kind and wealthy man that made his riches from agriculture. He is the owner of a dog named [[Togo]]. He lives in [[Boneshield]]. He is [[Montague Tepes]]’s friend. He is the last heir of the **Frey** family.

- Monty has a crush on him. [[Aurelia]] has also taken an interest in him.
- He purchased the [[Crooked House]] — his family's ancestral estate. After being nearly sacrificed there, he chose to **live in Boneshield town** rather than the manor, and gave the [[Boneshield Misfits]] the run of the cleared house as their base.

## The Petra Connection
Restoring his haunted family estate, Alistair combed the old Frey records and uncovered the **Tepes women** who had married into the family — **Petra** (wife of the Crooked Man, [[Crooked Man|Vlastimil Frey]]) and her mother **Kezia**. He was struck by how closely **Petra's portrait resembles [[Montague Tepes|Monty]]**, and so became the one to first tell Monty that something ties him to the Crooked House — through his **maternal (Tepes) line**, not the Freys.

> **Alistair and Monty are not related.** Alistair is a Frey; the resemblance runs through the Tepes blood Petra carried. He is the *messenger* of the mystery, not part of it. *(See [[Crooked House]] § The Petra Question.)*
