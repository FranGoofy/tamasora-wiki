A venerable gnome credited with the creation and establishment of the city of [[Astraloftum]]. He has a tomb in St. Ode'bbej Square in [[Astraloftum]], guarded by his immortal spirit guard [[St. Chopin]].

Some accounts suggest that Saint Ode'bbej and **[[Jebbedo Ode'bbej]]** — engineer, shipwright, and member of the [[Weastern Pirates]] of [[Aigora]] — are one and the same, separated by a portal in the Dividing Plains and an untold number of years. Neither record has been confirmed against the other.
