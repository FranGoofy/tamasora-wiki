An elderly, wise gnome and the spiritual leader and protector of the [[Whispering Grove]] in [[Astraloftum]]. Elowen has a deep connection to the [[Great Natu Tree]] and is revered for her profound knowledge of ancient druidic rituals and her ability to communicate with the spirits of nature. Her robes are adorned with symbols of the tree and the moon, and she carries a staff carved from a branch of the [[Great Natu Tree]] itself. She also specializes in leatherworking and woodcrafting.

She has perished and her Soul now resides in [[Finley]]'s Staff.
