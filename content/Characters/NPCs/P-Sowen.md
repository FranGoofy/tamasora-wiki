An [[Automaton]] that serves the [[Soul Shepherds]], knowledgeable about [[Rubix]]. 

It has a [[Sol Phone]] embedded in its head that communicates with [[Vincent of Damocles]], revealing that Vincent is spying on the Scions. He ends every question with “... Question?” Or “...Pregunta?”

While the Soul Shepherds explore [[Jupiter]], [[Beppo]] and the automaton P-Sowen have been left temporarily in charge of the Project [[A.R.K]].
