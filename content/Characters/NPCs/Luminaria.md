An ancient and majestic Elder Light [[Dragons|Dragon]] of the [[Trinity]]. She officiates the final trials for rookie [[Solar Scions]], inducting them into the order. She was previously the Queen of the Light Dragon [[Luminaria Kingdom]] many centuries ago, but something made the Kingdom fall to ruin and now she roams the skies looking for worthy Solar Scions and seeking [[Paradise]]. 

[[Carter Moonwing]] has a crush on her.

[[Argahax]] is her favorite word.
