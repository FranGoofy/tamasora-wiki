One of the noble sub-courts of the [[Gardens of Arcadia]], founded by an offspring of [[Amenó]]. The Spring and Orchid Courts are considered one and the same.

The Court of Spring was the sole supporter of the [[Court of Ash]] upon their arrival in the Gardens, ushering them in and providing resources to help them survive.
