*"We do not seek the light. We bring it."*

**SABER** is a clandestine unit of [[Solar Scions]] that operate outside the direct command of the [[Ecclesia Solari]]. Founded in the aftermath of the Invasion of [[Astraloftum]], they exist in the shadows of the Holy [[Trinity]]'s bureaucracy to handle threats that the Church cannot officially recognize – particularly threats that stem from within. *“The blade before the hymn”* they specialize in infiltration, soul-intelligence gathering, and [[Apostate]] termination. They report only to the High Priestess of the Ecclesia Solari, [[High Priestess Cetra]], and act as a necessary counterbalance to the idealistic image the Solar Scions project. Their goal is not finding [[Paradise]]; it is making sure the Church keeps its’ hold on the world. 

A shattered sun overlaid on a closed eye, representing silent vigilance and the willingness to destroy false light to protect the true Dawn.

**Founding.** After [[Amiel Tenebris]]’ disappearance, the Ecclesia Solari sent out some Solar Scions to search for him, they did not find him but they did stumble upon: ***“The Scriptures of False Rites”***, desecrated holy scripture believed to be from Paradise written by a group of Apostates calling themselves ***“[[Cult of Nirvana]]”***. Within them were encoded accounts of corrupted Scions; Apostates who became demonic infiltrators within the Ecclesia, falsifying miracles used to rally political power. **[[Saint Ascon]],** laid the groundwork for this secret society of Scions to ensure the survival of the Trinity’s truth – even if that meant waging a silent war from within.

**Structure & Protocols.**
* Operatives are referred to as Sci-Ops.  
* Members retain all official Scion privileges but operate as if **excommunicated**.  
* Possess a magical "Twilight [[Brand]]" which allows them to pass through Mirror Realms without losing their [[Souls]] (developed from research on [[Silas Clemens]] when he was younger)  
* Each operative undergoes a rite called the **[[Light Severance]]**.
* They are allowed to lie, kill, or abandon allies \- so long as the Dawn survives.

**Base of Operations.** [[Mirror Realm]].
**Memebers.** [[Thirteen]] A secret Scion assumed dead, now believed to be the Dawnbreakers’ most lethal operative.
