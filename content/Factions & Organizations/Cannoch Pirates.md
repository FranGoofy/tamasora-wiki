Named after their Captain [[Cannoch]]. Traveling alongside the [[Soul Shepherds]]. They are Wanted Pirates by the [[Golden Navy]] and have a bounty on their heads for theft, murder, piracy, and desertion. 2000 GP for Captain Cannoch’s head and 1000 GP for his first mate [[Fern]]. 

The only two remaining are Cannoch and Fern, the rest of them perished on [[Saturn]] during the [[A.R.K]] crash landing and subsequent [[Eruption Rain]]s.
