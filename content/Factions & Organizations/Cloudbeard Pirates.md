The Cloudbeard Armada is by far the most infamous [[Sky Pirates]] group of the common era. It is led by Admiral [[Cloudbeard]]. Notable members are: [[Air Hjorgin Stormson]], [[Sven Stormson]], [[Gunhild Stormson]], [[Zenith Aastrika]].

Other infamous pirates under the Cloudbeard banner wings are: Captain [[Cannoch]], [[Duchess Zeph]], Jeremiah ‘[[Goldscale]]’ Astor, and [[Rainbow Mania]].
