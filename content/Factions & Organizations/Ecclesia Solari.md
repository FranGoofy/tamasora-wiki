> *The one who seeks will find an answer. Do not turn them away. The Trinity grants answers to all who seek, yet the foolish do not see the answers laid out in front of them.*

The **Ecclesia Solari** is the dominant religious and political power in the [[Skysea]], dedicated to the worship of the Holy [[Trinity]]: **[[Natu]]** (Goddess of Nature), **[[Magi]]** (God of Magic), and **[[Tekk]]** (God of Technology). Headquartered in the divine bastion of **[[Astraloftum|Holy City of Astraloftum]]**, the Church’s primary directive is to locate the lost realm of **[[Paradise]]** \- "The Land of the Gods" \- which vanished 300 years ago, and to purge the world of the insidious **[[Fomorians]]**. 

The Church teaches that the [[Trinity]] grants answers to all who seek them, though they maintain a rigid hierarchy and strict dogmatic control over information. While they project an image of benevolent guidance, internal strife and secrets often fester behind the polished white walls of [[St. Ode'bbej Cathedral]].

**Current Status**  
The Ecclesia Solari is currently recovering from a devastating [[Fomorian Invasion of Astraloftum]], orchestrated by the [[Cult of Nirvana]]. While the city was saved through the heroism of the [[Solar Scions]], the attack exposed vulnerabilities in the Church’s defenses and deepened the political rift with rival powers like the [[Mercantile Hegemony]] and the [[Martian Imperium]], however, under the leadership of [[High Priestess Cetra]], the Church remains steadfast, believing that the arrival of the [[Comet of Eden]] marks the beginning of a new crusade that will finally lead them to the gates of [[Paradise]].
