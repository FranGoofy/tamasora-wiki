The [[Apostate|Apostates]] of Nirvana, ‘*Guardian Angels of Desire*’, [[Cult of Nirvana]], are the sovereigns who act as executors of the [[Idea of Evil]]'s will, by virtue of their desires being intrinsic to the eldritch entity. They reign over [[Apostate|Lesser Apostles]] as masters and are responsible for facilitating demonic reincarnations during sacrificial ceremonies. They have orchestrated many significant events that have largely affected the course of humanity and the state of the universe. 

Each '[[Demon|Angel]]' is believed to once have been a previous [[Trinity]] Apostate, [[Solar Scions|Scion]], or ambitious individual who distinguished themselves enough to be reborn as the demonic sovereigns they currently serve as. Century-fold, they each attained their proper forms during the demonic event known as 'the [[Crowning]]'.

Operating through the labyrinthian doorways of the [[Mirror Realm]], the Apostates scour the [[Skysea]], [[Tamasora]], and the [[Timescape]] for individuals to [[Twilight Brand]]; Individuals with Ambition, a promising lure for the Throne of Nirvana. By worshiping the idea of an ‘[[Unborn Fomorian King]]’, they seek to enact the upcoming Centennial [[Crowning]] Event, a coronation of a new [[Unborn Fomorian King]].

**Organizational Structure.** The Apostates forgo cohesion and organization by design, instead embracing chaos and defying [[Fate]]. Their Ideologies: The concept of ‘[[Nirvana]]’. 

The Cult harbors a specific hatred for the [[Solar Scions]] and the [[Ecclesia Solari]], who serve as champions for the '[[Trinity|False Trinity]]'.

In the past, the [[Cult of Nirvana]] used to be called this.
