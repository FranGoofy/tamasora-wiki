# Bhan-Ember

The **Bhan-Ember** are a [[Dwarf|Dwarven]] resistance movement fighting against the tyrannical rule of [[Traitor King Dumac]] and the [[Cloudbeard Pirates]] on the [[Titan]] [[Jupiter]]. Their name translates roughly to "Ember of the Deep" in Dwarven — a reference to the smoldering defiance that refuses to be extinguished even under Dumac's boot.

## Ideology
The Bhan-Ember are led by a Socialist philosophy, seeking to return power to the people of Jupiter rather than to a monarchy or pirate warlord. Their stated goal after overthrowing Dumac is a **Constitutional Socialization** of Jupiter's governance, an idea championed by [[Editor Rupert]] of the [[Scribes]].

## Members & Leadership
- **[[Alfoz]]** – Foreman of the Bhan-Ember and overseer of the construction of a massive banner of [[Tekk]] in the Bhan-Ember Amphitheatre.
- **[[Ribny]]** – A Bhan-Ember Dwarf who was cursed with the [[Forvivlad]] affliction, later cured by [[Silas Clemens]].

## Base of Operations
The Bhan-Ember share the **Inverted Temple** deep in the [[Undershell]] with the [[Scribes]], accessible through the [[Deep Roads]]. The base includes a barracks, a medical tent, an alchemical library, and a war room tower.

## Current Status
The Bhan-Ember have suffered heavy casualties fighting [[Fire Giants]] loyal to Dumac and are receiving fewer recruits by the day. The arrival of the [[Soul Shepherds]] has reinvigorated the resistance — particularly after [[Silas Clemens]] proved that the [[Forvivlad]] curse can be cured, opening a new avenue for rescuing enslaved Dwarves from the forge-factories.

## Key Location
- **Bhan-Ember Amphitheatre** – Where the movement is constructing a towering banner of [[Tekk]], a symbol of their faith and defiance.
