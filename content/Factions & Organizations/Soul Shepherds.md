# The Soul Shepherds

*Active Adventuring Party — Formerly the Solar Scions*

The **Soul Shepherds** is the name adopted by the adventuring group formerly known as the Solar Scions, following the conclusion of the Fomorian Invasion of Astraloftum. The name reflects their evolved identity: less an arm of the Ecclesia Solari and more a sovereign force guided by their own conscience and the wellbeing of the Souls of the world.

## Members

### Core Party
- **[[Virtus Tenebris]]** – Captain. Legionnaire and devotee of [[Tekk]].
- **[[Diallos]]** – Cometborn Monk, Prophet of the Trinity.
- **[[Silas Clemens]]** – Dhampir Cleric/Sorcerer; Living Soul Phylactery.
- **[[Whimsical K. Beque ('Whimsy')]]** – Titanborn Paladin/Barbarian of Natu; the party's heart and cook.
- **[[Bartholomew Reginald Baldwin XXIX]]** (Arse) – Emerald Kobold Paladin/Bard/Warlock; newest full Solar Scion.
- **[[Thirteen]]** (Steve) – SABER operative and secret informant, now on probation with the party.
- **[[Milodious Aureon]]** (Milo) – Titanborn Bard of Saturn; joined during the Jupiter arc.

### Former Members
- **[[Carter Moonwing]]** – Chronurgy Wizard. Departed after enacting Jaz'Farrah's escape plan at the trial in Janus City. Currently at large with Jaz'Farrah. *Note: The Carter currently missing may be a Carter from an alternate timestream, swapped during the resolution of the Time Loop.*

- **[[Davi Greymane]]** – Inquisitor; defected early in the campaign and joined the Servants of Nirvana.

- **[[Pinot]]** – Groundskeeper and crewmember.

## Mission
Find [[Paradise]] and deliver the message of Elder [[Luminaria]] to all the [[Titan|Titans]] of Tamasora. Along the way, protect the innocent, challenge tyranny, and shepherd the Souls of the world toward their rightful rest.

## Current Location
**[[Jupiter]] — Undershell of Carapax.** The Soul Shepherds are allied with the [[Bhan-Ember]] and [[Scribes]] resistance, working to overthrow [[Traitor King Dumac]] and [[Cloudbeard]]'s hold on the Titan.
