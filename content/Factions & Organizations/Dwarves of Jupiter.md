*"As it is forged."*

In contrast to the [[Kekoa]], the [[Dwarf|Dwarves]] on [[Jupiter]] act as chroniclers and craftsmen. They shape artifacts and carve runes into the [[Undershell]] Mountains to honor all [[Titan|Titans]] & [[Titanborn]].
## The Shellwrights
These Dwarves are responsible for creating the **[[Islamorada]]** ("island armor"), special Titan artifacts said to extend the Titan's lifespans. They view their craft as a sacred duty.

## Beliefs & Biology
- **Set in Stone**: When Dwarves die, they turn to stone. They believe in the Goddess **[[Trix’Alleb]]**, Lady of Rock. Their graveyards resemble galleries of lifelike statues.
- **Petrification Immunity**: Due to turning to stone at death, they are immune to petrification and sometimes keep **Basilisks** as pets.
- **Titanblood Oath**: A dangerous ritual where a craftsman infuses a drop of the Titan's super-heated blood (magma) into their masterpiece. This often leads to a state of **[[Forvivlad]]**.

## Language
- **Forvivlad**: Dwarvish for desperation, anguish, or complete and utter despair of flesh and bone.
