**"Glidewings" Taxi Services.** It’s not uncommon for deliveries and non-flying/gliding humanoids to be ferried in Janus City by trained [[Glidewing]]. A Glidewing service flies up steep steps and through the aqueducts with strapped cargo. Occasionally, a poorly trained Glidewing might get distracted chasing a fluttery skirt or a dropped piece of meat.
