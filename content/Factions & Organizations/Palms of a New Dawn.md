# Palms of a New Dawn

*Solar Scion Adventuring Party — Led by [[Animus Tenebris]]*

The **Palms of a New Dawn** is an adventuring party formed by [[Animus Tenebris]] in the aftermath of the [[Fomorian Invasion of Astraloftum]]. It is a coalition of [[Solar Scions|Solar Scions]] and [[Solari Inquisition]] soldiers gathered under Animus's leadership, dedicated to pursuing twin goals: finding [[Paradise]] and breaking the temporal stasis that holds his fellow Scions — the **White Hand** — frozen in time.

## Formation
After the Invasion, Animus spent weeks in prayer, seeking [[Tekk]]'s guidance to quell the rage in his heart. Through this reflection, he found new purpose. He gathered Inquisition soldiers as his crew and set out independently from the [[Soul Shepherds]], determined to contribute to the search for Paradise by his own path.

*"The next time we meet, I will be the brother you once looked up to."* — Animus to Virtus, before departing.

## Current Mission: Damocles College
Animus's first destination is **[[College of Damocles]]** in East Sky, where he seeks the wisdom of a renowned **Chronurgy Wizard** — someone who might know how to unravel the magical stasis holding the White Hand frozen.

## Known Lead: Aphra
Animus informed Virtus that their father, [[Amiel Tenebris|Sir Amiel]], had a close friend on [[Venus|Aphra]] — an elusive **Aphrazonian** who may hold the key to his whereabouts. However, Aphra maintains a strict rule: **only women may enter.** This complicates pursuit of the lead without Virtus.

## The White Hand
The **White Hand** was Animus's former Solar Scion group, frozen in temporal stasis by the power of [[Carter Moonwing|Carter]]'s [[Tome of Time|Tome of Time]]. Animus feels personally responsible for their fate and drives himself by guilt and love for his former crew. He writes letters to them regularly and leaves them at their bedsides.

## Known Members

- **[[Syrax]]** — Ruby Dragon companion (previously affiliated; current status unclear)
- Inquisition soldiers (unnamed)

## Current Status
At large in East Skysea, heading for Damocles College. Last contact was when Animus departed Astraloftum after the Invasion.
