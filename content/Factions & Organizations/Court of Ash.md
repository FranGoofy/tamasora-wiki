An extinct noble house of the [[Gardens of Arcadia]], once led by the **Duke of Ash**. The Court of Ash were regarded as strangers to the Gardens by the other noble houses, having only one supporter — the esteemed [[Court of Spring]], who ushered their arrival and gave them resources to survive.

The **[[Monarchon]]** cursed the Court of Ash's lineage, forcing [[Finley]]'s father to protect him during a massacre. The Duke of Ash's lineage continues in [[Tamasora]].

*"The ashen blood whose dust settles in spring — is strength kindled by the soil of our ancestors.*
*It may scatter, it may wander, but it always returns to the earth, forever strong, and never afraid of its fate, of its rebirth."*
— Duke of Ash
