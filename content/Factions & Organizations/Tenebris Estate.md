The Tenebris Estate is a renowned lineage of warriors and explorers devoted to the [[Ecclesia Solari]]. The family has produced some of the most celebrated [[Solar Scions]] in history, most notably [[Amiel Tenebris]] and his children [[Virtus Tenebris]] and [[Animus Tenebris]]. The estate is watched over by [[Zariel Tenebris]], Lord Commander of [[Astraloftum]], in the absence of her husband.
