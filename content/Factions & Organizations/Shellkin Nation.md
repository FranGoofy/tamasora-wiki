The [[Titanborn]] of [[Jupiter]] refer to themselves as the **Shellkin Nation**. Their island rests on the back of the colossal turtle [[Carapax]]. 

## Culture & Leadership
- **Leadership**: Led by the **Shell-Sharazas**.
- **Carapace Tales**: Their origin story is carved on the ridges of the land.
- **Carapace Rite**: A ritual where Shamans commune with the Titan’s shell to interpret cracks as omens or messages.

## The Skywandering
Among the Shellkin, failure is not tolerated lightly. A [[Kekoa]] or [[Dwarf]] who cannot contribute to their people’s endless pursuit of perfection is first given time to recover. If they cannot, they are cast out. This process, known as **the Skywandering**, is both a punishment and an act of mercy. Many of these exiles become wandering mercenaries, artisans, or hermits.
