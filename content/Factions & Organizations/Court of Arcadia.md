The ruling court of the [[Gardens of Arcadia]], led by the Elven Demigod [[Amenó]], first-born son of [[Natu]]. These celestial beings surpass the elves in their dominion over the Gardens and even the [[Spirit Realm]], tasked with maintaining the balance between nature's untamed chaos and civilization.

The Arcadians embody the primal principles of [[Natu]], venerating her through unwavering dedication to preserving the sanctity of the natural world. It was this conviction that led [[Amenó]] to forsake the mundane world of [[Tamasora]], seeking refuge in Arcadia.

## Notable Members

- **[[Amenó]]** — Founder and first King of the Gardens.
- **[[The Vizier]]** — Val's magus, the most powerful mage in Arcadia. Dispatched personally only when situations are truly dire.
- **[[Ash Marshal]]** — [[Amenó]]'s timescape hunter, sent seeking across the planes to bring those who would harm Arcadia to justice.
