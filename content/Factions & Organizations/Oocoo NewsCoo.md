The primary source of global news in the [[Skysea]], employs [[Oocoo]] and works with the [[Scribes]]. **Oocoo NewsCoo**  write, organize, and distribute delivery newspapers and wanted posters around the [[Skysea]]. Their [[Cloudgulls]] wear hats to signify their employment and carry bags over their shoulders, just like a paperboy and a small bag that hangs on the neck to collect money from anyone that purchased the newspaper. They are the main source of news and information for people across the Skysea. 

**Oocoo NewsCoo** normally perch around a [[Zephyr]]'s mast or fly through until they are called to deliver the news. 
* They do not deliver newspapers to the [[Martian Imperium]].
* This branch pays handsomely for accurate maps of the surrounding Skysea and also will sell crude maps to the public for a hefty fee.  
* They also allow the writing and delivery of letters for a fee.
