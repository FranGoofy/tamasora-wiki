People who are born on a [[Titan]] are dubbed **Titanborn**, thus it is an ethnicity. They form a loose cultural alliance with a profound understanding of the [[Skysea]] and its creatures. They are skilled sailors, fishermen, druids, and mystics.
## Titan-Guided Society
Living on the backs and bodies of colossal [[Titan|Titans]], the Titanborn are guided by a council of elders chosen for their wisdom and experience. They possess a deep spiritual and druidic connection with the Titans. The leader of a given Titan is known as a **Shaman**. 

The Titanborn's knowledge of the natural world, the [[Skysea]], and its creatures is unparalleled. They have specialized druidic circles focusing on their respective Titan.
