a powerful trading empire that controls the vast majority of the [[East Skysea]]. They have a vast network of floating cities and [[Zephyr|Zephyrs]]. Their capital is **[[Damocles]].** They are skilled traders and engineers. The Military hand of the Hegemony is the [[Golden Navy]].

The Hegemony uses advanced top-secret engineering practices for their airships and their flying structures, granting them a significant advantage in travel, trade, and exploration. They maintain a secular stance, prioritizing wealth and business acumen.

**Conflict of Interests.** While they coexist with the [[Ecclesia Solari]] and regularly conduct business with them, the Hegemony disagrees with the Crusades against the [[Fomorians]], believing it to be a waste of resources that could destabilize the region. The Hegemony also works with the [[Titanborn]] occasionally.

**Exploration Guilds.** The Hegemony once started as a union of [[Sky Pirates]] and adventurers, and slowly became a governing power as they accumulated wealth trading. Daring explorers head to find [[Paradise]] on occasion in the hopes of striking rich, but not many return to tell the tales of the Forgotten Land.
