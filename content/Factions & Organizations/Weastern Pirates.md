**The Weastern Pirates** are a legendary group of pirates & adventurers led by the infamous [[Tohnkh]]. Their vessel was known as "**the Wind of the Weast"**. They were the founders of [[Weastworld]] in the world of [[Aigora]].

[[Tohnkh]], [Savina](https://wind-of-the-weast.fandom.com/wiki/Savina_Grimsbane "Savina Grimsbane"), Toppo, [Grargh](https://wind-of-the-weast.fandom.com/wiki/Grargh "Grargh"), [Red Heiring](https://wind-of-the-weast.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Heiring "Red Heiring"), and [Maxedine](https://wind-of-the-weast.fandom.com/wiki/Maxedine_Chadwin "Maxedine Chadwin"), the founding members of the crew, overtook the slave ship and made it their own. In Port Tormenta, the pirates enlisted the company of [Neko](https://wind-of-the-weast.fandom.com/wiki/Neko_of_the_Nen "Neko of the Nen"), Nessaj, [[Khemmis]] [[Khallis]], [[Saint Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]].

Their stories and history have been relegated to myth and legend, spoken and told by [[Sky Pirates]] all across the [[Skysea]].
