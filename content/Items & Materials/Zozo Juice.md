# Zozo Juice (15-Year Aged)

*Rare Trade Good — 200 gp — Found in the [[J.T. Holy Chest]]*

*A fiery red drink with thick orange bubbles that make it appear almost molten. It is more akin to coagulated blood than an ale, considering how thick it is. Bubbles crawl slowly to the surface through its sanguine depths. An extremely spicy drink that makes it near-unbearable to consume. Only an idiot would think this is a good idea.*

## Properties

**Highly Flammable.** The drink is so volatile that water and suffocation cannot extinguish it once ignited. Only a *Dispel Magic* effect can put out the fire.

**Constitution Save DC 18** to consume without ill effect.

**On Failure:** The consumer is immediately overwhelmed with agony. They suffer:
- Moderate to severe penalties to all ability scores (GM discretion based on severity of failure)
- Physical damage scaled to how badly they failed
- The **Weakened** or **Petrified** condition
- A burned tongue — no sense of taste for 1d4 days

**On Success:** The consumer gains:
- +10 bonus to Constitution, Strength, and Intelligence
- Temporary immunity to fire and poisons
- Duration capped at 48 hours, scaled to roll success

**Regardless of outcome:** Both successful and unsuccessful drinkers end up with a burned tongue and cannot taste anything for 1d4 days.

## Source
Found inside the **[[J.T. Holy Chest]]** — Sir Amiel Tenebris's personal heirloom chest — alongside his journal, partial airship blueprints, and a chart of Windhell Skies.
