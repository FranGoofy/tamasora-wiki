# Trinitas

*The Ark of Prophecy — Guardian of Astraloftum*

**Trinitas** is the name given by [[Diallos]] to the ancient, wooden **Ark** discovered beneath the Forgotten Chamber of the Forgehearth District in [[Astraloftum]]. Diallos used the **Bracers of the Blacksmith** to breathe life and sentience into the vessel, bestowing it with the name Trinitas.

## History
The Ark was discovered by the Soul Shepherds during the Fomorian Invasion of Astraloftum. Guided by one of Diallos' prophetic visions, the Scions led a mass exodus of Astraloftum citizens through the Golden Door of the Forgotten Chamber, fulfilling the prophecy of a messiah leading people to safety within a massive cavern lair where the ancient Ark was discovered.

## Abilities
The Ark is capable of housing large numbers of civilians and serving as a mobile sanctuary. After Diallos animated it, it was given sentience through the Bracers of the Blacksmith.

## Current Status
Trinitas remains on [[Astraloftum]] as its guardian, protecting citizens in the event of another invasion. It has not departed the city.

## Connection to the Forgotten Chamber
The Golden Door that led to Trinitas required three emblems of the Trinity to open:
- **Ring of Magi** (Carter's)
- **Medallion of Natu** (Finley's, from Archdruid Elowen)
- **Bracers of the Blacksmith** (Diallos')

These correspond to the slots labelled **Dawn, Zeal, Ruin**.
