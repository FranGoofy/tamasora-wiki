# The J.T. Holy Chest

*Heirloom of [[Amiel Tenebris]] — Recovered by [[Virtus Tenebris]]*

The **J.T. Holy Chest** (belonging to **Sir Amiel Tenebris**) is a Holy Mahogany Chest that once belonged to [[Virtus Tenebris|Virtus]] and [[Animus Tenebris|Animus]]'s missing father. It is a sacred family heirloom of the [[Tenebris Estate]]. Industriously indestructible and one of the finest pieces of storage craftsmanship in the Skysea. 

## History
After [[Amiel Tenebris|Sir Amiel]]'s disappearance, [[Vincent of Damocles|Vincent]] arranged its sale at the [[Scheppihn Auction House]] in [[Janus City]] — unbeknownst to [[Zariel Tenebris|Zariel]]. [[Sven Stormson]] outbid [[Virtus Tenebris|Virtus]], but the Soul Shepherds ambushed the pirates at the Aque-Docks. The chest was launched into the air by Carter's Fireball and caught by [[Diallos]] with his astral arms.

## Contents

### Sir Amiel's Journal
Four personal entries written by Amiel to Virtus and Animus:

**Entry One — Trip to Saturn.** Amiel describes attending a previous [[Quadrennial Meal Festival]], entering his famous **Hell's Chicken** and losing to a cloudfish stew. Includes the **Hell's Chicken Recipe** (scrawled in charcoal, edges burnt): two Abyssal Chicken thighs, three cracks of obsidian pepper, pinch of sugar and salt.

**Entry Two — Hometown on Jupiter.** Amiel recalls watching a [[Pōpō Pele]] match at the old arena in [[Hipolum-Dhrum]], writing from "The Heights" — his old home on Jupiter. He expresses a desire to take Virtus and Animus to see it someday.

**Entry Three — Aphra's Warrior Princess.** Before [[Zariel Tenebris|Zariel]], Amiel met a warrior princess named **Leira** on the Titan [[Venus]] (Aphra). She showed him her sword moves and kissed him once. He kept a **snake pin** she gave him in his coat pocket. He notes that both had greater destinies than being together — implying Leira is still alive on Venus, bound by duty.

**Entry Four — For My Little Warriors.** A personal letter to Virtus and Animus: *"I fight so you two can laugh and live in a safer world. Remember that. I carry you both with me, even when I'm out in the Skysea looking for Paradise, you're both always on my mind. –Amiel Tenebris"*

### Partial Blueprints
Delicately sketched diagrams for a **[[Project A.R.K.|Solar Scion Airship]]** — panels of solar-etched plating, engine concepts blending Rubix inscriptions with Titanborn-grown wood, and a **"Sunfire Keel"** that channels radiance to soothe turbulent winds. The margins bear a small annotation: *"For Amiel's final voyage."*

### Chart of Windhell Skies
A cloud-current map folded three times. Red ink marks an unmapped horizon with a single warning: *"Anchor here only if hope outweighs fear."* The Windhell Zone is far more perilous than lower air zones due to ice-cold air, high altitude, and violent storms. This chart will help the Soul Shepherds navigate it.

### 15-Year Aged Zozo Juice
*See [[Zozo Juice]]*  
A rare, fiery-red drink of extreme potency and flammability. 200 gp value, 2 lb.

## Significance
The journal's third entry is the first hard confirmation of **[[Leira]] on [[Venus]]** — a woman from Amiel's past who may hold information about his disappearance or his path to Paradise. The blueprints suggest he was designing or commissioning [[Project A.R.K.|a vessel]] for a specific final expedition. The Windhell Chart suggests his last journey may have taken him into one of the most dangerous and uncharted regions of the Skysea.
