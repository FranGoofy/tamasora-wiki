**Airships.** Every nation of [[Tamasora]] has one, as they are the primary method of transportation across the [[Skysea]]. The largest fleets in the Skysea belong to the [[Mercantile Hegemony]]'s [[Golden Navy]] and the [[Martian Imperium]]. 

The [[Ecclesia Solari]] sends out [[Solar Scions]] on their own vessels, and the [[Atlas Kingdoms]] all have similar designs. [[Titanborn]] sometimes leave their home [[Titan|Titans]] aboard chartered Zephyrs from privateers or on crafts of their own making.
