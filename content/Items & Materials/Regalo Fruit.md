**Regalo** is a fruit that grows solely on the [[Titan]] [[Saturn]] (Janus). It is prized for its succulent taste and healing properties.

## Properties
- **Wild Magic**: The fruit is often oozing with wild magic (Arcana DC 14).
- **Healing**: Serves as a base for the rarest of potions.
- **Memory Ceremony**: Eating the fruit allows the [[Children of Jas]] to recall memories remembered by Janus.

## Cultivation
- **Regalo Tree**: A subspecies of Mahogany. Its wood is valuable for furniture, airships, and **Spirit Carving**.
- **Control**: Exportation is heavily controlled by the [[Titanborn]] of Saturn. Taking it without permission is considered theft.
- **Madam Orlaxi**: A Tabaxi orchard-keeper at the [[Mahogany Orchards]] who performs "wing inspections" to prevent smuggling.
