# Project A.R.K.

*Prototype [[Zephyr]] — formerly owned by [[Vincent of Damocles]], now operated by the [[Soul Shepherds]]*

See also: [[A.R.K]]

**Project A.R.K.** (also referred to simply as *"the [[A.R.K]]"*) is a prototype airship built by [[Tekkhead]] Labs under [[Vincent of Damocles]]' commission. It was gifted to [[Virtus Tenebris]] and the Soul Shepherds in exchange for Virtus accepting Vincent's relationship with her mother, [[Zariel Tenebris|Lord Commander Zariel]].

## The Ship
- **Engine**: Powered by a [[Rubix Engine]] — a psionic, time-space manipulating cube that responds to the esoteric *Rubix* language. The engine was originally unstable and required expertise to control. It was later revealed to be powered by condensed soul gems: souls that are neither alive nor dead, harvested as energy sources.
- **Hull**: Initially constructed from standard materials, later repaired and upgraded with **Mahogany wood** from [[Saturn]]'s jungles by [[Beppo]], giving it improved resistance to water damage.
- **Power Gems**: Three **Serulinite Power Gems** were installed to restore the ship to full capacity after the crash on Saturn.

## Crew
The ship operates with a skeleton crew, originally assigned by Vincent as informants to keep him apprised of the Scions' movements. The crew includes:
- **[[P-Sowen]]** – An automaton crewmember, later revealed to contain a hidden Sol-Phone device used by Vincent to spy on the Scions.
- **[[Cannoch]]** – Former pirate captain, joined as local cartographer of East Skysea.
- **[[Pinot]]** – Groundskeeper of the Tenebris Estate, hired as part of the crew.
- **[[Beppo]]** – Panda inventor and shipwright, currently overseeing repairs on Jupiter and temporarily in charge of the A.R.K. alongside P-Sowen.

## History
- **Maiden Voyage**: Set out from Astraloftum heading north after the Fomorian Invasion.
- **Crash on Saturn**: The ship was destroyed when the Titan Janus's trunk seized it and crashed it into the jungle. Repairs took approximately a month, funded in part by the [[Jas-Sharaza]] Council of Saturn (35,000 gp) as payment for the Soul Shepherds ending the Time Loop.
- **The Spy Discovery**: Carter discovered a Sol-Phone device hidden inside P-Sowen's head, proving Vincent has been monitoring the Scions' communications since the voyage began.
- **Current Status**: Fully repaired and sky-worthy. Berthed at [[Kelda-Dhrum]] in [[Jupiter]]'s Undershell while the Soul Shepherds explore on foot.
