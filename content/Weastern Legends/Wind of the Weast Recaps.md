The Wind of the Weast campaign ran from March 2019 to March 2023. Over the course of 97 numbered sessions and one final reunion, a crew of former slaves, wanderers, and misfits sailed the [[Aigora|Aigean Sea]], founded a city, and saved a world from a god who wanted to drown it. Their ship was called the Wind of the Weast. Their captain was [[Tohnkh]].

---

## Arc I: Salty Beginnings
*Sessions 1–12 | March–December 2019*

The founding sessions. A slaver ship is taken. A crew forms. Port Tormenta introduces the [[Weastern Pirates]] to the world — and vice versa.

---

### Session 1 — Salty Beginnings | March 14, 2019

*Written in Tohnkh's Captain's Log:*

> *I had woken up and next thing I knew I was on a slaver ship heading to Campa'Doar! Luckily, with my wit, I was able to break out of my holding cell with the help of some other people being held captive, just like me: Alumier, Maxedine, Savina, Grargh, and Red Heiring. There was an elven lady with a short red pixie haircut also being held captive on the ship, but she disappeared, along with one of the ship's rowboats — where could she have gone? We found out through a dwarf named Garden and a troll named Toppo that the captain was being controlled with a medallion in his possession to do the bidding of some unknown entity. We instigated a mutiny on the ship's captain, Sanguiss Fargoer of the Royal House Fargoer of Tormenta, and took the ship Wind of the West. Maxedine kept one of the medallions on him. He also talks to a rock named Bellatrix and calls it his wife — I pay it no mind. Red Heiring forged some documents, and we renamed the ship the Wind of the Weast and entered Port Tormenta after a day of traveling. We're pirates now, and we have our own ship. Now let's go, crew — forward to adventure. Mwahahahaha!*

---

### Session 2 | March 20, 2019

The Wind of the Weast arrives at the Commonwealth of Port Tormenta on Storm Isle after two days at sea. The white lighthouses of Tormenta and the White Bridge connecting the city's two cliff-side isles come into view. The crew navigates dockyard bureaucracy — Red Heiring's forged documents smooth the way — and makes their way to the Wetfoot Inn, the beating heart of Tormenta's pirate community. There, they begin putting out word for a crew.

---

### Sessions 3–12 | March–December 2019

*Records from these sessions are sparse.*

Over the course of these sessions, the crew spend time in Port Tormenta and the surrounding Aigean Sea, building out the ship and expanding their numbers. [[Neko of the Nen]], [[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]], [[Khemmis]], and [[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]] all join the crew during this period, recruited at Port Tormenta. The crew are introduced to the medallions of Nathair — strange golden artifacts that hijack the will of their wearers — and begin to understand that the mutiny that freed them may have been one thread in something much larger.

### Session 58 — Palace Recon | July 27, 2021

The [[Weastern Pirates]] scope out the Imperial Palace ahead of the royal wedding and meet **Kalkstein Grimsbane** for the first time. They mark him immediately as a threat.

The crew splits up:
- [[Khemmis]], [[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]], and [[Savina Blannis|Savina]] sneak into Kalkstein's personal chambers and begin uncovering his secrets.
- [[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]] seeks out Razgaal to cast Death Ward on her ahead of the anticipated assassination attempt — and has an uncomfortable encounter with Kalkstein in the process.
- [[Tohnkh]] and [[Neko of the Nen|Neko]] mingle with the wedding guests, gathering intelligence.
- [[Khallis]] takes his seat with the Golden Council and learns that **Entei** and **Athenia** are planning to assassinate the emperor — and want Khallis to join them.

### Session 59 | July 31, 2021

The [[Weastern Pirates]] regroup and compare notes, trying to make sense of the tangled politics of the Sang-Lothor Imperium.

[[Khallis]] communes with his warlock patron and discovers the truth: his patron has been **Nathair** all along, for years, without Khallis's knowledge. Nathair reveals he has consumed Entei and promises Khallis the chance to kill the emperor himself.

Meanwhile, [[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]] and [[Tohnkh]] go to warn the emperor of Nathair's plot. They are let in — but find no emperor. Instead they find **Kalkstein Grimsbane** posing as if he has committed the murder himself. Cere detects his thoughts and confirms he didn't find the emperor either; the throne room was empty when he broke in.

A fight breaks out. The rest of the [[Weastern Pirates]] and [[Khallis]] converge. Kalkstein is cut down — [[Savina Blannis|Savina]] landing the killing blow, driving a blade into her adoptive brother's chest. From his body, a many-eyed raven emerges. Savina catches it. Cere opens the bag of holding.

---

### Session 60 | August 14, 2021

[[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]], having spent the night thinking, makes the connection: the old man hiding in the wardrobe and calling himself Entei is the emperor himself. She relays this to the group.

Guards rush the throne room with **Athenia** and the 'emperor' in custody. [[Khallis]] impersonates the emperor. A confrontation and psychological standoff follows. Athenia is killed. The actual emperor falls unconscious and is in the crew's custody.

[[Tohnkh]] realizes that [[Khallis]] is a changeling. It distresses him, Cere, and — by some accounts — Khallis himself.

### Session 61 | August 17, 2021

[[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]] devises a plan to use the existing succession: allow Prince **Xervisian** to leave with Razgaal calling the shots over the empire going forward.

With forty minutes until the wedding, **Danimus Blannis** arrives with news: a woman in red has entered the palace and gone to the dungeons, where the child Entei is being held. The crew descend to find the woman is an Efreeti, summoned when the child rubbed a lamp.

Battle breaks out. [[Neko of the Nen|Neko]] and [[Tohnkh]] fight fire elementals with water. Shrim (in T-Rex form) deals with the corrupted Shaopings. When the Efreeti attempts to plane-shift with the child, [[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]] uses a Gate scroll to follow. They kill the Efreeti and retrieve the child. Cere removes the Medallion from her; the ghost child erupts in flame.

Danimus loses a leg.

### Session 65 — Sul Joins | September 14, 2021

The crew travel to the Eastern Juth Mountains aboard Sir Delirious's airship, the Arca Noe, seeking the Athai Monks — followers of Nathair who may have intelligence on the planned assault on Port Tormenta.

On the airship, the crew meet **[[Ezhmaar Sul|Sul]]**, a Drow from the Queendom of Webberast in the Endless Mountains, who is also hunting these monks and offers his firepower.

Aboard the ship: [[Neko of the Nen|Neko]] and [[Savina Blannis|Savina]] practice marksmanship. [[Khemmis]] and Danimus spar. Desmond demands back his Naginta — Khemmis offers to fight for it; Khemmis wins easily. [[Tohnkh]] receives final piloting training from the captain of the Arca Noe, surprising everyone with his aptitude. [[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]] communes with Opteima and Eldath. [[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]] learns the theory behind integrating elemental engines to make the Weast fly.

They skydive off the Arca Noe onto the mountainside and meet a small tribe of Aarakocra who point them toward the monastery. On the mountain path, a sleeping cloud giant is disturbed by a fleeing Athai monk who accidentally causes an avalanche. The party disposes of the giant — [[Tohnkh]] as a giant scorpion delivers the killing blow — and shelters from the snow using the giant's corpse to plug a cave entrance. They find the dead monk in the snowdrift, along with a Nathair Medallion confirming they're on the right path. [[Tohnkh]] communes with the cold mountain wind and locates the monastery.

### Session 66 | September 19, 2021

Trekking through snow, the crew spot a fey-like dragon weaving between peaks and encounter two young monk children — a white tiefling and a human boy — in an argument. After calming them, the human boy, **Young Fang**, reluctantly agrees to lead them to the temple.

[[Ezhmaar Sul|Sul]] and Desmond push ahead. By the time the crew arrives at the temple, a Wall of Fire is already roaring from the upper floors. Battle erupts. [[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]] splits off and finds a magical urn labelled *Nathair's Ashes* — a halfling monk girl explains it was meant to contain Nathair's Champion, who has been released into the basement.

Jebbedo follows her and confronts the awakened champion, discovering a replica of the Aigali Hourglass in the process. The Abbot and his protégé Anaconda appear — and flee before the crew can stop them. Desmond shifts into dragon form to give chase.

---

### Session 67 | September 28, 2021

Desmond's pursuit draws the ire of the fey dragon spirit. The crew gives chase. [[Neko of the Nen|Neko]] fires a Seeking Arrow; the crew follow it to the basement, where [[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]] has been holding his own against the Champion of Nathair unassisted.

The crew lend a hand. Jebbedo breaks through rubble blocking a room full of trapped children — the Abbot left them as a distraction on his way out. [[Ezhmaar Sul|Sul]] lands the killing blow on the Champion. The crew scoop up the children and exit the collapsing temple.

The dragon battle rages overhead between Desmond and the spirit dragon. An avalanche. The crew shelter under a tree. Jebbedo, [[Savina Blannis|Savina]], and [[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]] fly clear of the snow. Cere tells the children to retreat to the ruins and hands Young Fang a pamphlet on Eldath. He takes it.

The spirit dragon reveals that Desmond killed his master — Desmond's wife, and Razgaal's mother. Cere and Savina negotiate a temporary truce: the dragon will fight the cult with them, then pursue his vendetta. He agrees.

Below, the crew chases the Abbot and Anaconda into a cave. [[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]] dispels their teleport, trapping them. [[Tohnkh]] polymorphs Shrim into a Red Dragon T-Rex. [[Neko of the Nen|Neko]] holds the exit. The crew corners the Abbot through a maze of tunnels — the spirit dragon using True Sight to locate the invisible Abbot. A combined assault of smites, dragon-breath, frost rays, and axe blows reduces the Abbot to a thread of life. He stuns [[Khemmis]] and [[Ezhmaar Sul|Sul]] and runs.

Then [[Savina Blannis|Savina]] — who has had enough — steps into the Ethereal Plane via her Horizon Walker ability and shoots him twice through the veil. The Abbot falls.

[[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]] takes his Medallion. It triggers the Abbot's remains to explode in necrotic energy. A slimy voice enters her mind — Nathair, taunting, announcing that the Abbot has taken his secrets to the grave. He possesses [[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]].

Cere cuts the monologue short. She reaches for Jebbedo and calls on Eldath — genuine, total faith — and divine intervention answers. A green hand exorcises Nathair from Jebbedo's body and drags him underground. Jebbedo falls unconscious. He is alive.

**Level 12.**

### Session 68 | October 5, 2021

The crew rest in the mountains. [[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]] sleeps in a swampbie's arms and visits the Womb of the Grave, where she finds the soul of the Abbot — real name Uscie — and conducts his judgment. From him she learns:
- Nathair is building a tower.
- The Athai monks were meant to meet him at the Tower to help construct it.
- Nathair's plan involves the Aigali Hourglass and the spirit of Baal dwelling in [[Khemmis]].
- Nathair refers to Khemmis as "The Stupid Blue Brother."

After extracting all she needs, Cere sentences the Abbot's soul to damnation. In his ash she finds a glass shard — touching it pulls her into a flashback of the day Nathair first tried to flood [[Aigora]]. She sees the event through the eyes of **Eeiton Prime**, who sliced the tsunami in half with his sword and shattered a moon to halt the tide. *"I knew you would attempt this again,"* he tells Nathair. *"I have faith you will be thwarted once more."*

When Cere returns to the Womb of the Grave, Opteima is stunned that Eeiton Prime expressed faith in their crew. She becomes Cere's first Cleric of the Blooming Domain.

---

Back in the waking world: the spirit dragon notices [[Neko of the Nen|Neko]]'s necklace. When she removes it from him, his form shifts. He becomes an Air Genasi she recognizes — her fiancé, **Tenshi**, who had vanished the morning before their wedding. She punches him in the face.

On the airship back to the palace, Cere hosts a tea party and debriefs the crew on her dream. Khemmis and Tenshi have a breath-holding contest; Tenshi forfeits for Khemmis's sake.

At the palace, the crew dress for an opera commissioned by [[Khallis]]-as-emperor dramatizing how the [[Weastern Pirates]] "saved" the "emperor" of Sang-Lothor. Partway through, Prince **Xervisian** is abducted by a mysterious figure. The crew investigates — and finds Verde, the councilman, dead of what appears to be Power Word Kill. The Crimson Enchantress is suspected.

[[Savina Blannis|Savina]] tells Razgaal what Desmond did. Razgaal is devastated. Savina makes the decision to stay in Sang-Lothor with her wife.

The rest of the crew say their goodbyes and return to the Wind of the Weast. [[Tohnkh]] plants a tree in the palace courtyard. [[Khallis]], Tenshi, and Blushidia come aboard. The crew begin planning: find Xervisian, stop the Lich Witch.

## Arc VI: The Tower
*Sessions 69–85 | October 2021–February 2022*

Something immense is rising off the coast of Storm Isle. A ghost ship hunting the hourglass. A tower that drives people mad. And at the top of it — a dead woman who wants to become a god.

---

### Session 69 | October 13, 2021

A new crew member: **Brient**, a Dhampir of mysterious abilities and still-mysterious motives.

[[Tohnkh]] communes with nature to locate the ghost ship. [[Khemmis]] catches an octopus for a party celebrating the main crew's return to the ship. [[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]] builds something for Shrim in his workshop. [[Khallis]] moves into Savina's old room and sets up his Magic Mansion (prize from the dance battle). [[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]] runs errands — checking in with Grandma Venus, Eldath, Opteima, and Weastkeep — and discovers that Port Tormenta has fallen under the control of the **Aigean Commerce Company**, which is shipping building materials to a mysterious location in the middle of the ocean. Weastworld is housing refugees.

At the party, [[Tohnkh]] organises a duel to learn more about Brient; Brient chooses [[Khemmis]] as his opponent. They exchange blows before Brient incapacitates Khemmis with Hypnotic Pattern.

The next morning, [[Khemmis]] communes with Baal and receives a vision: a flying ship attacked by a scaled creature above a mist. Moments later, the Weast sails into exactly such a mist. Two bodies in the debris. One is hauled up — a uniform from an Air Ship. [[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]] identifies a component on the body as a part needed to convert the Weast into an airship.

[[Tohnkh]] senses two undead at the bottom of the ocean. The crew retrieve them: minions of the Crimson Enchantress, lost on their way to Hobmonkey Cove. The crew dispatch them. [[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]] scrys on Xervisian: he is in the tower, being tortured, and the Crimson Enchantress is expecting the crew to come for him.

The crew decide to engage the nearest ghost ship directly — to learn how to fight one.

### Session 70 | October 18, 2021

The Weast engages the ghost ship in combat. The crew discover that the ship's aura dematerialises everything in its vicinity by converting nearby creatures to undead. They kill all but one creature — which escapes via Dimension Door — and the ghost ship dissolves on defeat.

[[Tohnkh]] searches the ship before it vanishes entirely and finds a chest containing a flute.

The crew reconvene in [[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo's]] workshop and plan. The Weast needs a week and a half to reach Weastworld. Port Tormenta continues feeding building materials to the tower. Opteima's Tear is somewhere in the Nathair Archipelago.

[[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]] visits the Womb of the Grave in her sleep and learns from Opteima that the Tear of Opteima will allow a Champion to *birth* Opteima into [[Aigora]] — giving her a physical presence on the material plane. Cere agrees to find it.

### Session 71 | October 19, 2021

The crew Transport via Plants to Baal's Rest on Storm Isle, where a Tortle has appointed himself guardian of the place. He tells them Shrim's brothers passed through looking for him — they continued toward Port Tormenta.

[[Tohnkh]] asks about the fastest way to the Nathair Archipelago. The Tortle suggests petitioning his Dragon Turtle deity for passage through the Elemental Plane of Water. Tohnkh does so. The Dragon Turtle obliges.

The crew travel through the Plane of Water, besting a hostile hunting party of Tritons and a water elemental along the way. The surviving Tritons lead them to the **City of Glass** — inhabited by jellyfish oozes, Sahuagin, and Merfolk. [[Khemmis]] befriends a jellyfish in the market who gives him magic boots he cannot afford. [[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]] is charmed by a Hag oracle and nearly led astray — [[Tohnkh]] and [[Khemmis]] intervene. The Hag warns of *"a short lifespan."* Cere is unmoved.

The crew reach the jellyfish's home waters — and find him about to sacrifice himself against a sea monster. Together they defend him. The Letter from the Future that Khemmis has been carrying reads *"someone who has been friendly to you will die"* — then, after the jellyfish survives, the letter changes. The warning dissolves.

Cere takes a lesson from this: divination cannot fix what free will can alter.

The session ends as the crew arrive at the Nathair Archipelago's shore and bid the jellyfish farewell.

The Weast is caught in a trap: seven ghost ships forming a ring around her, the storm holding the ship in place at the eye. Using Animal Shapes, the crew sneak aboard the ghost ships.

The crew breach the iron box. Inside is a dungeon organized around the seven deadly sins. They fight bubbles capable of casting Fireball, then the floating lich skulls **Pride** and **Sloth**. [[Khemmis]] defeats both with the halberd looted from the Crimson Enchantress.

Further in, they find a door they believe leads to Nathair's chamber. They begin breaking it down.

The crew wake. The lich skulls return and attempt to steal [[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere's]] bag of holding — emptying its contents across the floor. [[Khallis]] teleports back to Weastkeep. [[Khemmis]] is banished to a maze.

The crew are teleported involuntarily: Cere and [[Tohnkh]] end up in the Womb of the Grave; [[Neko of the Nen|Neko]], Savina, and [[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]] remain inside the iron box — which Cere and Tohnkh realise is now somewhere inside the Womb of the Grave's mountain.

Cere spots Lust trying to escape. [[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]] spots the **Deck of Many Things** among the spilled bag contents and pulls ten cards.

One of them is a Fates card. Jebbedo uses it to undo Nathair's existence.

## The Alternate Timeline
*Sessions 93–98 | May–July 2022*

Removing Nathair from history is not the same as solving the problem. The crew wake up in a world that doesn't remember who they are — and something worse than Nathair has stepped into the vacuum he left behind.

---

### Session 93 | May 17, 2022

The removal of Nathair from history fractures the timeline. The former [[Weastern Pirates]] are scattered:

- [[Tohnkh]], [[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]], [[Khemmis]], and [[Neko of the Nen|Neko]] reside in Looselaw, which was never liberated.
- [[Savina Blannis|Savina]] lives in seclusion with Razgaal and Desmond, neither of them affected by the Blannis curse.
- [[Khallis]] remains in Sang-Lothor, at the palace, dining with the emperor and Blushidia. **Blushidia proposes to him.** He accepts. Moments later, **Eeiton Prime** crashes his ship into the castle. Only [[Khallis]] escapes.
- [[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]] continued living in Tal'Dorei — his home — for several years after the timeline split, memories of the previous life intact, before returning to [[Aigora]] on a mission to reassemble the crew. He speaks with Eeiton Prime and learns that the elder god plans to reclaim rule over Aigora alongside the Crimson Enchantress.

Eeiton tries to charm Jebbedo's son Pinocchio. Jebbedo teleports immediately to Cere and restores her memories, Tohnkh's, Khemmis's, and Neko's — handing them back everything they had lost.

---

### Session 94 | May 31, 2022

This session focuses on [[Savina Blannis|Savina]] and her family in the alternate timeline. She, Desmond, Razgaal, and their daughter **Mia** are celebrating Mia's eighth birthday when the sky begins to rain blood. Mia is kidnapped.

Desmond, Savina, and Razgaal teleport to Port Tormenta at Cere's request via Sending. On arrival, Razgaal and Savina lose consciousness from exhaustion.

Offshore, Team Looselaw approaches in the **Weast 2.0**, flying toward Tormenta.

---

### Session 95 | June 7, 2022

The Weast 2.0 approaches Port Tormenta through red rain. What appear to be meteors begin striking the ship — they are not space rocks but alien monsters. The crew kill them and push forward. A beam of red light extends from the moon. [[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]] dispels the Red Rain spell over the area.

Back in Sang-Lothor, [[Khallis]] attempts to ignite a rebellion. After losing patience with the resistance, he casts a Meteor Swarm of his own — then attempts to teleport to Port Tormenta in response to Cere's request. He ends up on the moon instead.

---

### Session 96 | June 15, 2022

The [[Weastern Pirates]] regroup in Port Tormenta, minus [[Khallis]]. [[Ezhmaar Sul|Sul]] and Brient have come to Desmond's aid to defend the unconscious Savina and Razgaal. [[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]] works with Sul and Brient to restore their memories. [[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]] evaluates Savina and Razgaal and determines that divine intervention is keeping them unconscious — she prays to Eldath, and everyone is healed.

Memories restored, the crew teleport to [[Khallis]] — finding him on the moon, in a waiting area with Mia, who disappears the moment the crew arrives. Khallis's memories are returned. The chamber fills with Sangromancer guards and more alien monsters. A Blood Witch and Blood Herald enter.

---

### Session 97 | June 26, 2022

The [[Weastern Pirates]] are defeated by the Crimson Enchantress and Eeiton Prime.

[[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]] pulls time-travel shenanigans again. The party emerges victorious.

---

### Session 98 — After the Weast | July 4, 2022

The [[Weastern Pirates]] return to [[Weastworld]] after having saved [[Aigora]]. They rest.

## Epilogue
*Session 111 | March 9, 2023*

---

### Session 111 — The Tournament of the Weast | March 9, 2023

The [[Weastern Pirates]] hold a televised tournament on a secluded island west of [[Weastworld]] — a contest to decide who is the strongest pirate on [[Aigora]].

Teams: **Khemmis, Jebbedo, Khallis, Cereus** vs. **Neko, Tohnkh, Savina**.

[[Khallis]] opens with a Meteor Swarm and levels the island. [[Tohnkh]], enraged, transforms into a giant volcano beast. [[Neko of the Nen|Neko]] becomes a gargantuan water elemental and faces off against [[Khemmis]]. [[Savina Blannis|Savina]] and [[Jebbedo Ode'bbej|Jebbedo]] duel in the forests. [[Cereus Aizlewood|Cere]] provides support from the back.

The island is destroyed. The fight is spectacular. The denizens of Weastworld cheer from every vantage they can find.

After the tournament: a parade at [[Weastworld]]. Travelers from across [[Aigora]] attend. The [[Weastern Pirates]] are celebrated, one final time, in the city they built.
