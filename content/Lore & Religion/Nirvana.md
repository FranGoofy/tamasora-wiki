‘Nirban’, ‘Atman’, lead to the release from the cycle of Rebirth (Samsara). Practiced by the [[Cult of Nirvana]]. The absolution of the [[Souls|Soul]].

The Cult of Nirvana preaches through whisper dogma of stripping away the false [[Trinity]]’s teachings, revealing to [[Mortal|Mortals]] & [[Fomorians|Fomorians]] alike that there are no [[Trinity]] and no Souls; that Rebirth, Life-Everlasting, comes from their very denial.
