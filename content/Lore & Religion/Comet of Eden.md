An important astronomical event in the world of [[Tamasora]], the texts of the [[Trinity]] inform that it happens every 100 years, and that the year it happens marks the start of a New Era. The Year of the Comet is said to bring about great change in the world and empower [[Solar Scions]] to insane levels at its’ conjunction; specifically, the moment of closest approach is often referred to as the Appulse of Eden. 

The [[Ecclesia Solari]] believes that the Comet of Eden is a sign of the alignment of cosmic forces with the prophesied Crusade. The enhanced powers bestowed upon the Solar Scions during this celestial event are viewed as a crucial element in fulfilling the prophecy of finding the Holy Land, driving out the [[Fomorians]], and ultimately restoring the world to its former divine glory.

In other cultures, such as the [[Children of Jas]] of Janus, the Comet of Eden may also be referred to as ‘Magi’s Comet’. Referencing the Celestial Observatory on Janus’ back that was used by followers of [[Magi]] (the [[Maskin]]) long ago to study the Comet.
