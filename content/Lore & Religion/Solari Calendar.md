**Months** established by the [[Ecclesia Solari]] but used by the rest of the world:
1. **[[Noctiel]].**		Named for the soul-reborn [[Noctu]] and the darkest phase of the moons  
2. [[Lunareth]].		Honors [[Lunareth]] and themes of envy, rebirth, and celestial sorrow  
3. **[[Everbloom]].**		[[Natu]]'s month; when balance, nature, and growth bloom in Calm season  
4. **[[Cindertide]].**		Marks the first stirrings of chaos as the Tempestuous season of the [[World Tempest]] begins 
5. **[[Ashfall]].**		Remembrance of destruction (e.g. [[Soul Wars]], [[Arcadian Phoenix]])  
6. **[[Stormwrought]].**	Named for the [[World Tempest]] and the wrath of the [[Titan|Titans]]

---

**Days** of the **Week**:
1. **[[Natusol]].**	Dawn, order, balance: Day of the Sun  [[Natu]]
2. **[[Tekktol]].**	Craft, engineering, challenge: Day of the Warrior [[Tekk]]
3. **[[Magidom]].**	Magic, insight, madness: Day of Knowledge  [[Magi]]
4. **[[Whimsday]].**	Light-hearted, Arcadian, Ephemeral Dreams: Day of Art 
5. **[[Virtuday]].**	Grit and work, rooted in survival and service: Day of Work  
6. **[[Dusquor]].**	Darkness, secrets, remembrance of [[Fomorians]]: Day of Grief  
7. **[[Samhain]].**	A day of rest, reflection, and prayer \- favored by all three Gods: Day of Rest
