Songs and music have long brought people together. Long sky based flights are no different. There is no better way to bring joy, unity and camaraderie to an Aircrew than through shared off-key singing. When Players and [[Zephyr]]s join in song, one of the Players in the group may make a Gain Morale Check.

**Cloudbeard's Bane** Come gather 'round, ye Shepherds true, / I'll sing the tale of a fleet we pursue. / The Admiral Cloudbeard, a dreaded sight, / With a pirate crew, causing endless fright. / With their stolen loot and Stormson's embrace, / They cut through the sky, at a terrifying pace. / They suddenly appear, with thunderous roar, / They come to steal our chests, and leave us poor. / From Janus to port, they plunder with power, / Commandeering ships, in their final hour. / Cloudbeard's Bane, this wretched fleet, / Our holy wrath burns, until their defeat. / If you hear a clap, in a clear sky, / Best ready your blades before ye die. / Through the stormy clouds, they swiftly fly, / Stealing treasures, from across the sky. / But the Soul Shepherds sail, elusive and sly, / To strike down the Admiral from on high. / So gather 'round, ye Shepherds true, / This tale ends with the pirate's due\!
