# Forvivlad

The **Forvivlad** (Dwarven for *"Stone-grief"* or *"Flesh of Despair"*) is a horrific curse afflicting the [[Dwarves of Jupiter]] under the reign of [[Traitor King Dumac]]. It is the result of prolonged exposure to toxic [[Titanborn|Titan]]-blood from spending too much time in the [[Deep Roads]].

## Symptoms
- Complete despair of flesh and bone — the infected's body begins calcifying, their skin hardening toward stone.
- Severe mental instability; infected Dwarves descend into madness and become prone to violent outbursts.
- Tainted blood that can be identified by [[Silas Clemens|vampiric sense]].
- In advanced stages, the sufferer loses autonomy entirely, making them highly susceptible to manipulation or direct command.

## King Dumac's Propaganda
Dumac refers to the Forvivlad as a *"blessing"* in his propaganda broadcasts, claiming the afflicted are *"elevated"* and *"become the indestructible Adamantine shields that protect our glorious kingdom."* In reality, it is the result of horrific exploitation and toxic working conditions.

## Cure
For a long time, the Forvivlad was believed to be incurable. However, [[Silas Clemens]] developed a bloodletting ritual that proved otherwise: by desanguinating an infected individual and replacing their corrupted Titan-blood with purified Dwarven blood from a healthy donor, the stone infection can be halted and reversed. 

[[Ribny]] was the first confirmed person cured of the Forvivlad, becoming living proof to the [[Undershell]] resistance that liberation from the curse is possible. This discovery has enormous implications for the [[Bhan-Ember]] rebellion.
