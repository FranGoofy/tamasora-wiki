A mysteriously ominous, massive egg found within the [[Atrium Profanus]] in the [[Mirror Realm]]. Its surface is composed of thousands of minute petrified human faces frozen in silent screams. Gray, blue, and dormant, rife with Unborn realities. When touched by one with "Great Ambition", the eyes on the surface are said to weep a viscous, black ichor.

**Destroyed by [[Silas Clemens|Silas]].**
