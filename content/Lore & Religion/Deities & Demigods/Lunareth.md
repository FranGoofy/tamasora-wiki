One of the two Moons of [[Tamasora]], visible throughout many [[Worlds]]; a [[Trinity|Goddess]]. 

She is the younger sister of [[Noctu]]; the two Lunar Goddesses.

One day, [[Lunareth]] killed her sister [[Noctu]] in a fit of jealousy; [[Natu]] became distraught and unleashed a Solar Fury on their village, disintegrating the chaotic sister. Unable to bring back lover [[Noctu]] back to life (as she had no [[Souls|Soul]] as destined by [[Natu]]).

[[Lunareth]] was then revived by [[Magi]] and ascended as a Lunar Goddess to keep [[Natu]]'s and [[Noctu]]'s celestial love in check.
