One of the two Moons of [[Tamasora]], visible throughout many [[Worlds]]; a [[Trinity|Goddess]]. 
The ascended mortal that became the **Lawful Lunar Demigod**. 

**Tale of Two Lovers.** [[Lunareth]] and her sister [[Noctu]] were once [[Mortal|Mortals]] dwelling in a village outside [[Paradise]]. As Mortals, they were forever barred from the celestial [[Paradise]] that the [[Trinity]] presided over.

One day, [[Lunareth]] killed her sister [[Noctu]] in a fit of jealousy; [[Natu]] became distraught and unleashed a Solar Fury on their village, disintegrating the chaotic sister. Unable to bring back lover [[Noctu]] back to life (as she had no [[Souls|Soul]] as destined by [[Natu]]).

[[Magi]], tainted by his Split Soul, furiously stipulated that mortals who ascended could not succumb to the bonds of love, for it would bridge the divide of Mortals & Gods, which disturbs [[Natu]]'s Cosmic Balance. Thus, [[Noctu]] and [[Natu]], driven by an eternal love, could only meet during a rare lunar eclipse, a fleeting moment where the gods' and mortals' realms briefly intersected. This tragedy mirrored the dance of celestial bodies – forever entwined but destined to be apart.

She is the older sister of [[Lunareth]]; the two Lunar Goddesses.
