The primary source of dismay in the world. After the loss of [[Paradise]], Souls have nowhere to go - instead, they begin to dissipate and lose their individuality before complete dissolution - they then turn into [[Spirits]], [[Undead]], or simply, Energy.

* [[Phylactery|Phylacteries]] store Souls. [[Silas Clemens]] is a living phylactery. 
* [[Demon|Demons]] hunger for Souls.
* The [[Spirit Realm]] and the [[Gardens of Arcadia]] are home to several *lost Souls*.
* The [[Great Natu Tree]] houses Souls, preparing them for the return of [[Paradise]].

**Properties of Souls.**  Souls have several interesting properties, such as:
* They can be regarded as [[Fey]] and [[Undead]]. 
* They cannot be seen by mundane mortals.
* [[Dwarves of Jupiter]]'s Souls remain in their Stone Forms when they die.
