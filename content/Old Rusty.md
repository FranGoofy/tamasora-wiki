The mute, eccentric groundskeeper who tends the Founder's Round in [[Boneshield]]. He has been around as long as anyone can remember, rarely speaks, and when he does it is in cryptic, hard-to-parse phrases. Recently, and strangely, he left Boneshield and wandered east to [[Foxwillow]].

The party came looking for him: the Mayor named Old Rusty as **the last person in Boneshield to see [[Aurelia's Hunter]]** before he vanished toward Foxwillow.

%%
**GM.** Old Rusty is **Frantz Frey**, son of the murdered servant Josip Frey, and so **kin to [[Alistair Frey]]**. *Calm Emotions* or careful Persuasion makes him lucid for one minute:

> *"Frantz Frey is my name. I was a boy. My ma told me to run. Tell Alistair. Tell Alistair we are kin."*

- **The trail:** he saw [[Aurelia's Hunter]] (the elf who used the name "Rumpus" and left the [[Silent Princess]] flower) and drifted after him toward Foxwillow. He can point the party to where the hunter has been lurking.
- **Long game:** Frantz is a living thread back to the [[Crooked House]] and the Frey/Tepes line. In [[The Crooked Nightmare]] he speaks the quest hook lucidly: *"The house on the hill dreams. The vermin scurry once more."*
