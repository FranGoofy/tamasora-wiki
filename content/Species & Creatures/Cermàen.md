**Kees-bàelar (whiskered-floater**) are huge airborne creatures, somewhat akin to a fish. It is considered a great luck and extreme rarity to meet one up close, though on a clear day one can sometimes see them floating far above, solitary or in twos or threes. They depend largely on the [[Skysea]] to keep themselves afloat, and are usually seen near the jet springs of the **[[World Tempest]]**, though they are known to migrate large distances, which is somewhat of a mystery. The creatures usually feed on huge quantities of insects, or an occasional unlucky bird.  
 As all the other syntonic animals, they have inherited two pairs of eyes; the second one is presumed to be weave-sensitive, however, whether it is still functional is not known.
