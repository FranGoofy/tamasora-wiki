Ancient beings that bargained for esoteric and divine knowledge and assistance. They were masters of wizardry far before [[Magi]], or [[Mortal|Mortals]] for that matter, before they arrived to [[Tamasora]]. 

They made some poor choices during their rise and fall. They aligned with [[Jupiter]] and [[Saturn]] during the [[Titan God War]]. They were just a little slow, and it caught up to them in the end.

Their City was melted under [[Mars]]' fire.
