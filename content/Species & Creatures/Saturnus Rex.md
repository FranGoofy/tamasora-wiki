# Saturnus Rex

*"The Tyrant of Saturn" — Apex Predator of the Jungles of Janus*

The **Saturnus Rex** is a colossal, fire-breathing dinosaur that serves as the apex predator of [[Saturn]]'s jungles, inhabiting a volcanic cavern on the Titan's body accessible through an ancient portal reinforced with obsidian runes.

A massive T-rex with volcanic properties — its roar alone is capable of freezing creatures in place with primal fear. Its body radiates intense heat, and the cavern it inhabits is filled with rivers of molten lava.

## Encounter with the Soul Shepherds
The Saturnus Rex was awakened when the Soul Shepherds entered its lair, seeking to clear it as a safe zone for [[Beppo]]'s airship repair operations. In the ensuing battle:
- It swallowed [[Silas Clemens]] whole, though its struggle forced it to regurgitate him.
- **[[Carter Moonwing]]**, under the influence of [[Magi]]'s command, drove a javelin through Silas's heart — ostensibly saving him from falling into lava below, but nearly killing him in the process.
- **[[Virtus Tenebris]]** strang[[Whimsical|Whimsical]]ue ('Whimsy')|Whimsical]]** delivered the killing blow with her cast-iron pan.
- The battle cost [[Silas Clemens]] his **left arm**, lost to the molten depths.
- [[Finley]]'s prayers to [[Natu]] revived Silas after the ba[[Whimsical|Whimsical]]ue ('Whimsy')|Whimsical]] extracted the **Saturnus Rex Heart**, which she meticulously marinated over several days and used as the centerpiece of her winning dish in the [[Quadrennial Meal Festival]].
- **[[Carter Moonwing]]** secured two unhatched **Saturnus Rex eggs**.
- Silas's lost arm was later restored by Carter using the **Magi Tablet**.
