The Dreamers are an extinct race of [[Titanborn]] humanoids from before written history. [[Isra]] is the last known survivor. They were known to be able to enter the **Plane of Dreams**.

Their ruins have been found throughout the [[Skysea]], in deep underground areas known as [[Dreamcaves]] within its' islands and Titans. Their legacy is largely lost to time.

## The Timeless Slumber

In the days before the [[Titan God War]] wiped out their civilization, along with the [[Snails]], a group of these "dreamers" foresaw the danger and came up with a plan to survive it. Time passes differently in dreams, and the ancients sought to use that fact to free themselves from the [[Timescape]]. Suspended between reality and dreams, sealed deep underground in the [[Titan|Titans]] in underground bunkers known as '[[Dreamcaves]]' they could live in a state of perpetual slumber for as long as necessary and outlast the disaster that would wipe out the rest of their kind.

The plan worked, but living for so long within the **Plane of Dreams** had unforeseen consequences. Upon waking, the dreamers found that they could no longer differentiate between dreams and memories, with both fading quickly from their minds. The result was the emergence of a new race with no knowledge of their own history, only half-formed images and dream-like impressions of a place and time that may not have existed.
