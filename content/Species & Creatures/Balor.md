# Xaparac, the Reflected Warden

*The Balor of the Old Palace. A demon of Mirror Jupiter, older in its loyalties than the Cult of Nirvana.*

For years, every forge in [[Hipolum-Dhrum]] burned on the fire of the demon chained beneath [[The Old Palace]]. The occupiers called it the **Demon Core**. The Church would call it a **[[Fomorians|Fomorian]]**. Its name is **Xaparac**, and it has never served the Cult of Nirvana, the Church, or anyone who wears a crown. It serves the Titan [[Carapax]].

## Born Behind the Glass
The [[Trinity]] built the [[Mirror Realm]] as a prison, "for demons, or something far older." Xaparac knows which. After the [[Titan God War]], the reflections of the punished [[Titan|Titans]] were sealed behind the glass, and demons like Xaparac were born from those reflections, the way [[Spirits]] are born from the Titans themselves. *(This is Xaparac's account. It cannot lie, but it can be wrong.)*

Xaparac was born in **Mirror Jupiter**, Carapax's shadow on the far side of the glass: a hollow, burning shell where the living Titan carries cities. It wears its Titan's name reversed, the way a knight wears a lord's colors. **Xaparac. Carapax.**

## Not One of the Crowned
- The [[Cult of Nirvana]]'s demons are **made**: mortals, Apostates, and fallen Scions reborn through the [[Crowning]]. Xaparac calls them **the Crowned**. Borrowed fire, bought with ambition.
- Xaparac was **born**. Never crowned, never branded. It will not kneel to an [[Unborn Fomorian King]], or to [[Vyvanix]], who rules the Mirror but not its loyalty.
- It spits on the word "Fomorian." A Church word, invented so the Titans' war could be blamed on whatever the faithful needed to hate.

## How It Came to Be Chained
- When the occupation's forges began drilling into and bleeding [[Carapax]], Xaparac came through the glass to end them.
- [[Zenith Aastrika]] was waiting. She drove a shard of her crown into its chest and chained it into the old royal furnace.
- She couldn't simply kill it: a demon of the Mirror that dies here only goes home. So she kept it and bled it, and for years its fire powered the machine that was killing its Titan.
- It broke free in [[SxS - Chapter 7#**Session 86.** Flame Let Loose|Session 86]], killed Aastrika, and swallowed her soul.

## Personality: It Cannot Lie
Everything in the Mirror is the opposite of the world. The world's holy men lie. **Xaparac cannot.**
- It speaks plainly and patiently, without flattery. Its threats are promises. Its offers are sincere.
- When it offered the Scions peace on the bridge, it meant every word.
- Lies disgust it. It hears one in a mortal voice the way a smith hears a flaw in a blade, and it never forgets who told it.
- **At the table:** everything it says, aloud or through Silas's mind, is true. That is what makes *"Yield, and I walk away"* and *"Kill me, and I only go home"* land.

## What It Wants
- **To go home.** Back to Mirror Jupiter, through a door or through death.
- **The forges dead.** Aastrika is ash. The rest of the machine should follow her.
- **No one digging.** [[Cloudbeard]] is excavating toward the [[Makina Spiralis]] and the [[Tellac Oranic]]. Either would wound Carapax again. Xaparac would burn the Undershell before allowing it.
- **Nothing from the gods.** It will never bargain with the Trinity or its Church. It might bargain with a Scion. It knows the difference.

## Relationships
- **[[Zenith Aastrika]]:** Its jailer. Dead, and swallowed.
- **[[Silas Clemens]]:** The one it chose to speak to. It can feel the Mirror-door in his brand.
- **[[Thirteen]]:** Hatched from a Mirror Realm egg. Xaparac recognizes glass-born kin, whatever the Church told him he is.
- **[[Diallos]]:** Raised the Totem of Janus in the Demon Core. A Titan's totem, lifted by a Trinity priest. Xaparac noticed.
- **[[Cult of Nirvana]]:** The Crowned. Contempt.
