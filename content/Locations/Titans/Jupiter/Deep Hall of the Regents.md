# The Deep Hall of the Regents

*Ceremonial Hall — [[Deep Roads]], [[Undershell]] of [[Jupiter]]*

The **Deep Hall of the Regents** is a solemn, ancient ceremonial hall within the [[Deep Roads]] of [[Jupiter]]'s [[Undershell]], accessible through a magical stone door requiring ten chimes struck with different weapons. The hall serves as a monument to the Dwarven Kings who shaped Jupiter's history, with each ruler preserved in stone effigy on a ceremonial pedestal.

## The Souls of the Kings
[[Silas Clemens|Silas]] detected that the Souls of the Dwarven Kings linger within the hall. With [[Whimsical|Whimsical's]] permission, he offered the Kings the chance to continue fighting for Jupiter's future. Two accepted:
- **Queen Orga the Deep-Seer**
- **King Morgan the Builder**

Their Souls now reside within Silas as willing passengers, adding to his collection of Soul-vessels.

[[Milodious Aureon|Milodius]] was disturbed by this act, though he did not intervene.

## Mysteries
- The **missing 1st pedestal** suggests a ruler whose name or legacy has been deliberately erased from history.
- The **removal of King Dumaclath's statue** — whether by [[Traitor King Dumac|Dumac]] himself or by loyal resistance fighters — is unexplained.
- The names etched on the **temple shaft** leading up to the [[Inverted Temple]] above are of fallen resistance fighters whose bodies were never recovered — a companion memorial to the hall's official record.
