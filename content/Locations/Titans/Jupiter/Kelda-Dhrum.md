# Sul Kelda-Dhrum (Kelda-Dhrum)

*Ancient Dwarven Fortress — Southern Undershell of Jupiter*

**Sul Kelda-Dhrum** (often shortened to *Kelda-Dhrum*) is a once-thought-abandoned Dwarven fortress located in the southern reaches of [[Jupiter]]'s [[Undershell]], accessible via one of [[Carapax]]'s orifices — a tunnel the [[Soul Shepherds]] discovered thanks to intelligence provided by [[Joy]].

## Description
The fortress is built into the body of Carapax itself, its walls formed from the Titan's calcified ribs and ancient stone. Massive **stalagmites of calcified rib** rise from the sludge that pools on the fortress floor. A superheated **Adamantine Door** with five circular valve-slots guards the deeper sections of the Undershell. The cryptic Dwarven inscription above reads: *"As Above, So Below, The Work is Never Done."*

## The Adamantine Door Puzzle
To unseal the door, five separate valve-rooms must each be triggered to fill the door's slots with lava. The valves must be activated in a precise order, as turning them causes toxic sludge to rise. The party solved the puzzle with the help of [[Beppo]], who raised the final lever remotely via [[Sol Phone|Sol-Phone]] while the rest of the Scions rushed through before sealing the door behind them.

## Inhabitants (on Arrival)
- **Forvivlad Dwarves** – Enslaved miners on the brink of madness, scouring every last vein of Adamantine ore. The Soul Shepherds convinced them to abandon the fortress after [[Whimsical|Whimsical]] destroyed the remaining ore veins.
- **Giant Abyssal Chicken** – A territorial creature that briefly captured [[Silas Clemens]], demanding Souls as tribute.
- **Vorthropod** – A magma-dwelling creature that erupted from the lava when the first valve was pulled.

## Current Status
The fortress has been cleared and serves as the docking point for the [[Project A.R.K.]], now under the watch of [[Beppo]] and [[P-Sowen]] while the Soul Shepherds venture deeper into the Undershell.
