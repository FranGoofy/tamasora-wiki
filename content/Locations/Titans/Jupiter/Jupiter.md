The Ancient **Carapax**, a massive sized [[Skysea]] Turtle. A [[Titan]], Carapax has witnessed all of history: the worst of Mortals, and the best. Its’ body is a monument to the rise and fall of civilizations. Carapax has a massive wound (the ‘Valley’) in the center of its’ shell, a scar from when it defied the [[Trinity]].

## Geography & Society
- **[[Shellkin Nation]]**: The [[Titanborn]] of Jupiter.
- **[[Undershell]]**: A sprawling, subterranean continent within the Titan's ribcage.
- **[[Kekoa]]**: Burly hunter-gatherers.
- **[[Dwarves of Jupiter]]**: Chroniclers and craftsmen.

## Current Events
The [[Cloudbeard Pirates]] are in control of Jupiter. They are starving the common folk and forcing them to work in factories to build weapons. [[Cloudbeard]] has enforced his rule with a Titanwide Winter Storm known as the [[Gales of Ikaros]], forcing Carapax into hibernation. He is searching for a legendary weapon deep in the [[Undershell]].

## Creation Myths
- "The Carapace of Carapax cracked during the God Wars, and from its fragments, the first Shellkin emerged."
- [[Tekk]] ("war table" in Dwarven) is regarded as the God of the Earth and Underworld.
