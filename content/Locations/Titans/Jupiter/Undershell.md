Beneath the colossal exterior of the [[Titan]] [[Carapax]] lies the **Undershell**, a sprawling, subterranean continent encased within the Titan’s massive ribcage and armored plating.

## Environment
In ages past, it was a place of balanced geothermal warmth lit by bioluminescence. Now, under the rule of the [[Cloudbeard Pirates]], it is a suffocating, hyper-volcanic inferno.
- **Internal Weather**: Super-heated winds and a perpetual downpour of gray volcanic ash and toxic [[Orichalcum]] smog.
- **Architecture**: A blend of brutalist stone-carving and massive, chain-suspended forge-cities built into the bone and shell.
## The Tyrant's Reign
The Tyrant Dwarven King **Dumac**—in a pact with the Cloudbeard Pirates—has driven siphon-drills and blast-furnaces into the Titan's magma-valves. 
- **Core Chips**: Volatile fragments of pure Titan-force extracted from Carapax, sold to the [[Martian Imperium]].
- **Feverish Agony**: The extraction causes Carapax’s internal temperature to rise dangerously, threatening the entire ecosystem.
