Sealed deep underground in the [[Titan|Titans]]. These are ancient bunkers for [[Dreamers]]. Here, they could live in a state of perpetual slumber for as long as necessary and outlast the disaster that would wipe out the rest of their kind.

# Known Dreamcaves

1. In the [[Undershell]] of  [[Jupiter]]:  In [[Lake Ruthaba]] contains a **Dreamcave** that the eldritch entity [[Abathur]] found.
2. On [[Neptune]] in [[Yahar'gul]]: Where [[Isra]] awakened. 
3. On [[Saturn]], below the [[Elephant Spire]].
