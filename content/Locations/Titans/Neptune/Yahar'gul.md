# Yahar'gul

**Yahar'gul** is a region in the [[Atlas Kingdoms]] of the [[Skysea]] on the Titan [[Neptune]]. A land shrouded in dense mystical forests and sweeping valleys. Once ruled by [[Count von Preshons]] — Nirvana's Drakebane, Fallen Lord of Yahar'gul. The land has fallen into a deep mist; its' inhabitants dispirited, and the land, ripe for the taking.

It was the birthplace and early prison of [[Silas Clemens]], where he endured years of torture under his father's design to forge him into the [[Unborn Fomorian King]].

[[Boneshield]] is a town within its' region.

Yahar'Gul has the following features if you wish to keep the characters trapped and alone:
- **In the Mist**. No magic, such as [Plane Shift](https://5e.tools/spells.html#plane%20shift_xphb) or [Teleport](https://5e.tools/spells.html#teleport_xphb), can transport a creature beyond the borders of ***Yahar'Gul*** without being shepherded by a Stranger.
- **Crooked Grasp**. Magical travel out of ***Yahar'Gul*** is made impossible by ancient wards. When a creature tries to exit, it gets lost and ends up being teleported to a forest within the valley.
- **Cut Off.** Any attempts to communicate magically with creatures outside of  ***Yahar'Gul*** automatically fail.

## [[The Fall of Yahar’gul]] 
A military campaign led by the [[Ecclesia Solari]] and the [[Solar Scions]] to dismantle Count von Preshons's stronghold. The mission succeeded in rescuing the young Silas from his father's clutches, and the Count was slain.
[[Holy Mother Anastasia]] personally rescued Silas during this siege, bringing him back to [[Astraloftum]] and raising him within the [[Solari Abbey]].
