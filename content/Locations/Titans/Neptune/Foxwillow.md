Foxwillow is the farming community east of [[Boneshield]], the breadbasket of the valley: grain, dairy, and livestock for the wider region. Lately the bread and dairy have stopped coming. Crops fail, herds sicken, neighbors turn on one another, and a **scarecrow that walks** has been seen at the edge of the eastern cornfields. People who wander into the corn do not come back.

The party travels here to find out why (and to follow **Old Rusty**, last seen heading to Foxwillow, who is the trail to [[Aurelia's Hunter]]).

# The People of Foxwillow

- **William Lodge** — a warm, well-liked farmer and the town's good heart. Quick with a bad pun and a kind word; he once petitioned to make the scarecrow Ol' Jericho a citizen. He left town to seek aid in [[Boneshield]] when things turned dire.
- **Martha Langtree** — a widowed farm matron, brash and beloved, a "force of nature." Raising three children: Adelaide, Isaac, and young Rosemary. She is frantic: Rosemary vanished a week ago, and Isaac went missing just last night.
- **Jonah Sawyer "Heels"** — Foxwillow's butcher, broad-shouldered and big-humored, in bloodstained apron and platform shoes. His daughter **Liza** has gone missing. Lately guilt-ridden, defensive, and short with his neighbors.
- **Adelaide Langtree** — Martha's eldest (25). Ol' Jericho's old best friend. Since he vanished she walks into the cornfields nightly and comes back wrong. The town whispers about her.
- **Isaac Langtree** (21) — ran the family tavern, the Hooded Lantern. Vanished the night before the party arrives.
- **Rosemary Langtree** & **Liza Sawyer** — the two missing girls, taken into the corn.

# Foxwillow in Flames (the Burning Mill)

The party arrives to the town square ablaze. A dozen "farmers" brawl in **Magpie Square**; they are actually **scarecrow townsfolk (Pumpkin Heads)** animated by Raum, faces hidden under hats and bonnets. The **Old Windmill** is a spinning tower of fire.

- **The brawl began with Martha.** Frantic over her missing children, she started a fight in the windmill that the curse turned murderous. **Jonah is in danger of dying** in the mill.
- **Save Jonah** from the burning mill to make **Martha** instantly Friendly. Douse the **General Store** and **Town Hall** before the packed square (and every scarecrow in it) burns down. There is a water pump west of the square.
- **The feud keeps the scarecrows animate.** While the Martha and Jonah grudge burns, the Pumpkin Heads keep rising. Calm or reconcile the townsfolk and the scarecrows collapse.

# Investigating Foxwillow

Once the fire is out, the survivors talk (attitude gates what they share):

- People only vanish after walking **into the eastern cornfields**, often seen **whispering to the stalks** first.
- Ol' Jericho, the beloved singing scarecrow, vanished about six months ago; the town has been accusing each other of his "murder" ever since.
- **Adelaide** wanders into the corn nightly and returns unbothered while everyone else who follows is lost.
- **Isaac's body** lies in the windmill cellar. Telling **Martha** that Adelaide killed him breaks her heart, and she gives the party a brass locket Isaac made for her as a child. *(Isaac's Locket = a Cloak of Protection.)*

This investigation reveals **how to enter the Fields of the Crow**.

# The Fields of the Crow

East of town, the cornfield with unnaturally tall stalks is the threshold to **Raum's demiplane**. Those who approach of their own will are bidden to **pay a secret** to enter; forcing through only spits you back out.
