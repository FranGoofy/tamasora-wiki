The askew manor on the hill - the old **Lockwood Estate**, ancestral home of the **Frey** family - where the Freys were murdered and [[Vessla Browntooth]] brewed souls in her cauldron. Cleared by the [[Boneshield Misfits]] in Session 5, it is now their **base of operations.**

When [[Isra]] struck Vessla down, the house's 'crooked geometry settled true, the conservatory storm stopped, the teeth underfoot blew to dust, and the portraits un-twisted. The freed Frey dead went quiet for good. It is no longer haunted — but it remembers being crooked.

## Base of the Misfits
Traumatized, [[Alistair Frey]] kept ownership but lives in [[Boneshield]], giving the Misfits the run of the manor. 

The rooms have sorted to their tenants: the **Conservatory** to [[Riqirith]] (her thriving **bergamot tree** and creatures), the **Library** to [[Aurelia]] (her curse-tome), the **Calliope Alcove** to [[Isra]], the **Trophy Room** to [[Montague Tepes|Monty]]. **Mr. Buttons**, the sentient teddy bear, declined to leave and remains its quiet sentinel.

## What Still Lingers
- **Wisp lives.** The [[Veilcat]] escaped and still prowls the grounds wearing **Walter Jenkin's stolen voice**. Until he's killed, Walter stays mute. *(Open side-quest.)*
- **The Crooked Man — vanished, not destroyed.** **Vlastimil Frey** ([[Crooked Man|the Crooked Man]]) was nowhere to be found. Killing Vessla can't end him — only **burning the house and severing the Frey line** can. Unburned, the house *still dreams;* he leaves only signs (a half-heard rhyme, a wrong-angled shadow). *(Foreshadows [[The Crooked Nightmare]].)*
- **The sisters named.** Scratched in the floorboards: **Stonoga Blackstinger. Golub Graygullet.** — the other two [[Wraithcoil Coven]] sisters, who felt Vessla die.

## The Petra Question *(clue)*
**Petra**, Vlastimil's late wife, bears an uncanny resemblance to **Monty** — yet he has no kinship with the Freys. The link is blood: **Petra was born a Tepes** (as was her mother, Kezia) and married *into* the Freys. She's an older branch of the **same Tepes line** Monty descends from via his mother, the Countess [[Evelyn Eve Tepes]]. What ties him here is his **maternal line**, not the house.


**GM note:** long-game foreshadowing — Petra → the Tepes bloodline → the Countess → [[Count von Preshons]] and the half-brother Monty doesn't know he has ([[Silas Clemens]], a Scion). Let it haunt him; don't explain it.
