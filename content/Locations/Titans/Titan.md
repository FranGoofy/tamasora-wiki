The Titans are giant divine beasts that roam the [[Skysea]] of [[Tamasora]]. They are ancient beings that predate written history. Long ago... the [[Trinity]] and the Titans lived side-by-side. Then everything changed, when the [[Idea of Evil]] came to be, thus begiging the [[Titan God War]]. 

[[Mortal|Mortals]] born on Titans are called [[Titanborn]].
