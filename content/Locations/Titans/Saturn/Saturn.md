**Janus** is a massive, ancient elephant [[Titan]]. Its back is an island, its legs are pillars of the world, and its presence defies time and space. It is older than words, possibly as old as the [[Trinity]].
## Geography & Climate
- **[[Jungles of Janus]]:** Magically wild and untamed jungles filled with prehistoric beasts (dinosaurs, large cats, gorillas).
- **[[Eruption Rain]]**: A twice-daily flood of cloudwater and salt that cascades from Janus's trunk.
- **[[Janus City]]**: The main settlement, sitting halfway up the shoulder ridge.
- **[[Elephant Spire]]**: A massive spear-like tower piercing the Titan.
## Society
- **[[Children of Jas]]**: The [[Titanborn]] of Saturn.
- **[[Jas-Sharaza]]**: The shamans who lead the Children of Jas and can hear Janus's thoughts.
## Lore
- **The Penitent Beast**: Janus is said to be carrying the Elephant Spire as penance for sins against the Old [[Trinity]].
- **The Jas-Sharazas**: Only they can feel the echoes of Janus's breathing and chart its eternal course.
