The **Elephant Spire** is a massive tower that resembles a spear piercing the [[Titan]] [[Saturn|Janus]]. It is considered an ancient wound suffered by the Titan by the [[Trinity]].
## Lore & Mystery
- **Penance**: Janus is said to carry the Spire as penance for sins against the Old [[Trinity]].
- **The [[Maskin]]** reside here.
- **Island Rubix Cube**: Saturn’s [[Rubix Cube]] is located inside the Spire.
- **The Call**: The Spire hums at night, a resonant pulse that seems to be reaching for something beyond the [[Skysea]]—possibly [[Paradise]].
## Quotes
- *“I carried it once. It burned me. It burns still.”* — Janus
