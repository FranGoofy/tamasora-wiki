**Janus City** sits halfway up the shoulder ridge of the great Elephant [[Titan]], [[Saturn|Janus]]. It is a tropical, mountainous, and incredibly wet city.
## Population & Culture
- **Population**: ~8,000 (swells during festivals).
- **Dominant Species**: Winged Humans, Winged Gnomes, Winged Half-Elves, and Aarakocra (considered the "purest" children of Janus).
- **Culture**: Theocratic, ruled by the [[Jas-Sharaza|Jas-Sharazas]]. Known for fierce local pride and zesty fruit-based recipes.
- **Language**: A variant of "Titan," often accompanied by chirping and squawking.

## Key Locations
- **[[Freedom's Respite]]**: A large inn tower and tavern.
- **[[Mahogany Orchards]]**: A dual-purpose orchard-lumber yard producing [[Regalo Fruit]].
- **[[Beppo Co. Aeroworks]]**: An airship workshop owned by Beppo, an eccentric Panda inventor.
- **[[Bellow Springs]]**: A winged bathhouse fed by [[Eruption Rain]].
- **[[Scheppihn Auction House]]**: Built from mahogany Titanwood and basalt.
- **[[Elefanti Palace]]**: Where the Jas-Sharaza Council convenes.
- **[[Le Festin Row]]**: The city's market and square, home to the [[Quadrennial Meal Festival]].
- **"The Fall" Hall**: A seedy ravine for gliding bets and punishment.
## Infrastructure
- **Aqueducts ("Ribbons")**: Funnel [[Eruption Rain]] into "Hydro-Balloons" for storage.
- **Step-Stone Neighborhoods**: Balconies and huts clinging to stone "ribs" of the Titan, linked by rope bridges.
- **Flying Lanterns**: Gourd-lanterns filled with luminescent pollen.
- **[[Glidewing Taxi Services]]**: Large, birdlike reptiles used for transport and deliveries.
