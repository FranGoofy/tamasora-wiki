A dual-purpose orchard-lumber yard with terraced farmland with large, specialized paddle-wheels capturing [[Eruption Rain]] to drive sawmills. [[Regalo Fruit]]: Grows in clusters on broad-limbed, vine-laced trees. The orchard’s main orchard-keeper, Madam Orlaxi, a Tabaxi, is known for delivering “wing inspections” to ensure smugglers aren’t carrying contraband fruit in their feathers.
