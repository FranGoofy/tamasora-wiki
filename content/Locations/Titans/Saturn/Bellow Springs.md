Local bathhouse fed by [[Eruption Rain]], famed for “aerial steam rooms” where winged folks roost overhead to enjoy ephemeral warm thermals. Misted illusions occasionally appear in the steam, reflecting half-formed memories from [[Saturn|Janus]]’s ancient backlog. Bathers sometimes see glimpses of old visitors. Visitors might glean cryptic clues about Janus’s regrets. Or an assassin might hide behind these illusions, attacking the unsuspecting.
