# Elefanti Palace

The seat of government for the [[Jas-Sharaza]] Council of [[Janus City]] on the [[Titan]] [[Saturn]]. The Palace is where the Council convenes, communes with [[Saturn|Janus]] through the **Eruption Pool**, and passes judgment on matters of law and faith.

## Architecture
The Jas-Sharaza deliberately allow the forces of nature to consume the Palace's structure — vines overtake the walls, moss fills the joins, and roots push through the floors — as a living embodiment of Janus's passage of time. A **stone staircase shaped like an elephant's trunk** lowers like a drawbridge to admit visitors.

## Key Features
- **The Eruption Pool** — A sacred pool connected to Janus's inner essence. [[Reina Akaska]] oversees it. [[Silas Clemens|Silas]] secretly dove into the pool (with [[Talleah Preshons|Talleah]]'s help) and communed with Janus directly, learning that the [[Elephant Spire]] is still an active beacon — pointing toward [[Paradise]] (or what Fomors call "Nirvana").
- **The Courtroom** — Where the Trial of [[Jaz'Farrah]] was held. The site of Carter and Silas's dramatic escape plan and the shattering of the Soul Shepherds fellowship.

## The Trial of Jaz'Farrah
The five Jas-Sharaza Elders who presided over the trial were eccentric, silent robed figures who moved quickly toward execution. [[Diallos]] and [[Bartholomew Reginald Baldwin XXIX|Bartholomew's]] combined advocacy delayed the verdict, but the Council ultimately sentenced both Jaz'Farrah and [[Talleah Preshons|Talleah]] to execution — before the chaos of the escape plan intervened.

## After the Trial
Despite the disruption, the Jas-Sharaza Council honored their debt to the Soul Shepherds for ending the Time Loop and freeing Janus from the [[Elephant Spire]]'s loop-beacon. They funded the full repair of the [[Project A.R.K.]] (35,000 gp).
