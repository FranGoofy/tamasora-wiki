The interior of the Scheppinh Auction House owned by [[Amadi Scheppihn]] overlooks a broad, open-air Veranda that faces a grand statue of [[Saturn|Janus]]’ trunk made of [[Titan]] Ivory; the trunk symbolizes the offering of the boons of the [[Skysea]]. From this vantage, you can hear distant chanting and occasionally glimpse feathered shamans bustling about.

**Architecture.** Built from mahogany Titanwood pillars, reinforced with carved basalt blocks. Decorative inlays feature swirling motifs reminiscent of elephant tusks and trunk-spirals, paying homage to Janus.

**Sky Bridges.** Two narrow sky-bridge walkways connect the Auction House’s top floor to neighboring terraces and aqueducts, providing dramatic entry points for VIP guests.
