A small settlement on the outskirts of the Upper Body [[Jungles of Janus]]. Population is small, only about 100 residents. Most of the villagers here are farmers, bakers, and woodcarvers. They belong to the original [[Maskin]], before the great Maskin Schism happened. Now they are impoverished and Mayor-less.

●     The [[Soul Shepherds]] helped them by providing some much needed coin.
