Known as “The Highway of the Skies”, this [[Air Zones]] is the standard transport level for all [[Zephyr|Zephyrs]]. The altitude range of this zone makes for ideal conditions to maneuver and travel at a good pace. You will find all manner of vessels at this altitude, from [[Sky Pirates]] Junkers on the hunt, to family air caravans setting off for their summer holidays.

**[[Flying Islands]]**. 

**Inhabitants**. The [[Mercantile Hegemony]] and travelers dominate this zone, constantly sailing from one city or island to another. While it’s a common passageway, occasional pirate raids do occur, especially targeting less protected vessels. 

Pirates who dare to operate here are often either desperate or ambitious, as the [[Golden Navy]] keep a close watch. Pirate raiders who operate here prefer hit-and-run tactics, attacking weaker or isolated airships before escaping into the lower Skysea Zone. These pirates are cunning, using storm clouds or sky debris to ambush their prey.
