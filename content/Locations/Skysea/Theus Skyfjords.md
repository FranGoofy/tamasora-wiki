# Theus Skyfjords

*Impassable Mountain Range — South [[Skysea]], between [[Saturn]] and [[Jupiter]]*

The **Theus Skyfjords** (also called the **Thorianna Skyfjords** in some charts) are a vast, impassable mountain range stretching through the Skyway air zones of the South Skysea. The mountains are perpetually ice-covered, with temperatures plummeting to extreme cold even in the Tradewind zone above them.

## Navigation
The Skyfjords must be climbed over rather than flown through — the mountains are too dense for lateral passage. The Soul Shepherds ascended through the Zephyrs air-zone into the Tradewind Zone to crest the peaks during their journey from Saturn to Jupiter.

## The Giant Ambush
The Theus Skyfjords were the site of a brutal surprise attack by the **[[Cloudbeard Pirates]]**, who used the mountains as cover to ambush the [[Project A.R.K.]]:
- **[[Gunhild Stormson]]** ("Runemaiden") — Stone Giant Corsair, attacked from one side with Abyssal Hyenas and four Hill Giant Goons. Polymorphed into a turtle by [[Milodious Aureon|Milo]] and sent gliding into the Skysea.
- **[[Air Hjorgin Stormson]]** — Ice Giant Corsair, attacked from the opposite side with a second boarding party. Fought [[Virtus Tenebris|Virtus]] in a brutal duel before triggering a **massive avalanche** that buried Virtus and left her with a permanent facial scar. Died in an honorable final duel with [[Bartholomew|Bartholomew]], requesting with his last breath that Bartholomew tell Admiral [[Cloudbeard]] the tale of their legendary fight.
- **Freezle the Measle** — Enlarged Kobold arcane fuelman. Killed himself and almost took Thirteen and Diallos with him via a Contingency Fireball.

## Rescued During the Ambush
- **[[Joy]]** — Scribe of the Shell; rescued from captivity aboard the enemy vessel *Misty Stalker*. Missing her left arm; heavily scarred; reunited with [[Whimsical|Whimsical]] (who she did not initially recognize).
- **[[Milodious Aureon|Milodius]]** — Titanborn bard; held captive and forced to play battle music for the giants. Freed himself using restored magic after the anti-magic shackles were removed.

## Loot Recovered
From the destroyed Cloudbeard vessels:
- 1,500 gp of unrefined Orichalcum
- 1,000 gp of Adamantine Ore
- Two Potions of Enlarge
- Two Potions of Reduce
- Scroll of Chain Lightning (given to [[Silas Clemens|Silas]])
- Air Hjorgin's Helmet and Boots (taken by Bartholomew in honor of the fallen Giant)
