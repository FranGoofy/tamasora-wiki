While some smaller flying islands drift into the [[Skyway Zone]], these islands are often seen as **waypoints** or stopping areas. They usually have bustling port towns or refueling stations where [[Zephyr]] crews can stop for supplies and repairs. Merchant cities and trade hubs also exist in this zone, suspended high in the sky, like [[Damocles]], flourishing with trade and commerce. They are well-defended, deterring pirate attacks.

As a contrasting example: [[Floating Islands]] are stationary.
