Legends speak of an unfathomable [[Water Sea]] hidden far beneath the clouds, some [[Rubix Cube|Rubix Cubes]] call it ‘*[[Aigora]]*’. 

Adventurers who dared plummet beneath the [[Skysea]] never resurfaced; airship crews speculate the depths might hold primordial creatures or portals to realms unknown. 

An airship captain may seek brave [[Solar Scions]] to test an experimental submersible airship, designed specifically to penetrate the clouds and uncover the truth beneath. 

The Skysea has no surface, no bottom. Anyone or anything that falls through the clouds ends up in the [[Sea of Death]].
