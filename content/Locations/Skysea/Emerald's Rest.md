Emerald's Rest is a naturally fortified town, as an ancient Emerald Dragon took its final breaths in this area. It lies within the [[North Skysea]], northeast of [[Mars]], home of the [[Emerald Kobolds]] and ancestral home of [[Bartholomew Reginald Baldwin XXIX]], located in the [[Draconic Graveyard]]. 

It is one of many Kobold enclaves. There are factions within [[Kobold|Kobolds]] who believe that the [[Solar Scions]] will save the enclaves from the threat of the [[Martian Imperium]] while others deny it wholeheartedly stating the fact that they are considered monstrous.
