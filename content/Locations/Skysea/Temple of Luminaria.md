*Flying ruins of a forgotten kingdom*

Shrine of the Solari Elder Light Dragon [[Luminaria]] in the eastern [[Skysea]]. Summoned through the Mirror Tower Ritual: aligning a set of mirror towers to form a shining viscous pool. Final-trial site for rookie [[Solar Scions]]. The rookies also met [[Agoya]] and [[Lumi]] here, and [[Finley]] befriended the pegasus [[Augustus]].
