[[Astraloftum]]'s orphanage, located within the [[Solari Abbey]]. Children who have lost their parents due to illness, war, or [[Fomorians]] live and learn here, experiencing a childhood full of love.

Run by [[Holy Mother Anastasia]], a [[Tiefling]] priestess and foster mother to [[Silas Clemens]].
