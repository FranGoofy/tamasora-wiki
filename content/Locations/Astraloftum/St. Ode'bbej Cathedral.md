An enormous, towering white cathedral at the heart of [[Astraloftum]], visible from anywhere in the city. This is where [[Solari Sermons|sermons]] are read, people pray, debate holy scripture, store ancient relics, and where [[High Priestess Cetra]] and the rest of the [[Ecclesia Solari]] Church Hierarchy reside.

## Sub-locations

### [[Hall of Scions]]
The headquarters of the [[Solar Scions]], where missions are handed out and Scions are tracked.

### St. Ode'bbej Square
The central gathering point for the populace of [[Astraloftum]]. Home to the Tomb of Saint Ode'bbej, guarded by [[St. Chopin]], his immortal spirit guard. Festivals and announcements by the [[Ecclesia Solari]] are held here.
