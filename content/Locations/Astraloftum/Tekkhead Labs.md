Situated beneath the [[Forgehearth District]] of [[Astraloftum]], Tekkhead Labs is where [[Tekk]]-blessed inventors push the boundaries of technology. Sentient automatons serve as advisors and companions, and a deep network of tunnels exists where Gems are mined. Rival inventors vie for commissions from the Church and competitions are staged here. There are several clearance levels; what exactly happens in the deeper levels remains a closely guarded secret.

[[Vincent of Damocles]] owns an entire lab here - where he was secretly building the [[A.R.K]] and [[P-Sowen]].
