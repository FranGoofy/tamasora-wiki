Located in [[Astraloftum]], the Sun Docks are where Solar Sailships restore their energy using the radiant power of the Sun. Other airship engine designs are also supported for travelers without Solar Sailships.

The Sunweavers — revered for their ability to harness solar energy with delicate mirrors — work around the docks. Solar Sailships undergo intricate "Skywashings," cleansing them of impurities and replenishing their sails with solar energy. The [[Solari Inquisition]] Customs roams the docks, cataloging what comes in and out to ensure the safety of citizens.

Whispers speak of a hidden fleet of warships, dormant within the docks, ready to be unleashed in the name of the [[Trinity]].

## Refugee Crisis
There is an ongoing refugee crisis at the Sun Docks. A convoy airship from the [[Atlas Kingdoms]] arrived full of refugees from war-torn nations. Mostly Humans and Halflings — Half-Orcs are not being allowed in easily.
