A district of [[Astraloftum]] where the development of Solar [[Zephyr|Zephyrs]], holy [[Automaton]], and weaponry takes place. Numerous taverns and inns serve pilgrims as part of their pilgrimage to the Holy City. The night is filled with vibrant hustle and bustle — open-air markets offer goods from across the sky realms, while taverns echo with divine music and tales of aerial voyages.
