A sacred natural place within [[Astraloftum]] where the druids of the Circle of [[Natu]] reside. The air is filled with the divine chirping of **Avieres** — celestial birds said to be musicians of the gods who accompany Druids on errands and aid in the preservation of the Grove.

## Symphony of Ascension
An elaborate [[Soul Shepherds|Soul-Shepherding]] ritual that can only be conducted once a full moon, when the veil between [[Natu]] and her lover [[Noctu]] is thinnest. The love between the Goddess and the demigod creates a powerful essence used to shepherd souls from people who have died on [[Astraloftum]], depositing them deep into the [[Great Natu Tree]] until [[Paradise]] is found again.

The Grove is closed off to all visitors during the ritual and a protective barrier is raised. The movement of souls attracts Lunar Demons to the tree, which the Druids fend off. The ritual involves Sylvan chanting in tune with Aviere whistling and the playing of ancient instruments crafted from the wood of the [[Great Natu Tree]].

## Myconid Colony
Myconids rumored to have tended the Natu Forest since before even the Circle, granted sentience by [[Natu]] herself. Honorary members of the Circle.

- **Fungar the Protector** — Stout guardian myconid, current leader.
- **Lumi, Keeper of Light** — Vibrant myconid known for bioluminescent properties.
- **Mycelo and Hyphina** — Young sporelings, the new generation.
- **†Sporaxis the Wise** — Former elder and leader, recently deceased. Assigned Fungar as successor.
