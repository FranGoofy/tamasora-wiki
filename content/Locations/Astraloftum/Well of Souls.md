Located at the deepest roots of the [[Great Natu Tree]] within [[Astraloftum]], the Well of Souls is both a nursery and sanctuary for souls that cannot pass on to [[Paradise]]. It is a place of immense spiritual power, mirroring the energy of [[Natu]] and [[Noctu]]'s divine love — ancient scriptures say [[Natu]] would reunite with [[Noctu]] during full moons in a place reminiscent of the Well.

Those who serve [[Natu]], near the end of their lives, pass their knowledge and essence into the tree, ensuring their contributions endure within the Well.

## Current Status
During the [[Fomorian Invasion of Astraloftum]], the Well became corrupted and overflowed with tormented energy, shooting a necrotic beam upward through the [[Great Natu Tree]]. The [[Soul Shepherds]] intervened: the Corrupted Souls were absorbed into the phylactery known as '[[Dark Silas]]', whose husk was placed in Temporal Stasis by [[Carter Moonwing]]. The Well is now sealed again within the husk. The people of [[Astraloftum]] can no longer commune with their ancestors through the tree, causing widespread grief and concern.
