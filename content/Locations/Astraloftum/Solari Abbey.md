Nestled in the Magi Crags that rise behind [[St. Ode'bbej Cathedral]] in [[Astraloftum]], the Solari Abbey is where Monks meditate and train their bodies and minds. Within its tranquil gardens, monks cultivate rare sky-flora, each bloom humming with mystical energy for healing or enchantment. The Abbey is led by [[Abbot Muho]].

[[Diallos]] grew up here.

## Sub-locations

### The Scriptorium
A repository of lost languages and ancient sky maps, holding secrets of forgotten civilizations. Here, books are written, works of art are created, and [[Solari Sermons|Sermons]] are transcribed.

### [[Natu's Starlit Nest]]
Where children who have lost their parents due to illness, war, or [[Fomorians]] live, learn, and experience a childhood full of love. Run by [[Holy Mother Anastasia]].
