Located within [[St. Ode'bbej Cathedral]] in [[Astraloftum]], the Hall of Scions is the headquarters of the [[Solar Scions]], where missions are handed out and Scions are tracked.

## Sub-locations

### Map Room
The main strategic coordination center of the order, located within the Hall. The Sol Communications Terminal is located in the center of the room.

### Sanctum of Memory
A room inside the Hall containing weapon relics and other artifacts of deceased [[Solar Scions]], as well as memorials and chronicles of their deeds.

### Scion Hangar
Exclusive parking space for Solar Sailships.
