The Great Natu Tree is an ancient tree over 300 years old and the last of its kind from the now-forgotten [[Paradise]]. Located within the [[Whispering Grove]] in [[Astraloftum]], it serves as a [[Phylactery]] for [[Souls]] that have nowhere to go, housing them in the [[Well of Souls]] until the day [[Paradise]] is found again. Residents of [[Astraloftum]] use the tree to communicate with their lost loved ones.

The Druids of [[Natu]] — the [[Whispering Grove#Archdruids|Circle of Natu]] — revere and protect the tree. The inside was sealed by the Church 40 years ago until the next Lunar Eclipse; only [[Solar Scions]] may venture inside during the [[Whispering Grove#Symphony of Ascension|Symphony of Ascension]] on January 3rd.

## Current Status
Following the [[Fomorian Invasion of Astraloftum]], the sickened Great Natu Tree was saved by the [[Soul Shepherds]]. The Corrupted Souls overflowing within the [[Well of Souls]] were absorbed by the phylactery known as '[[Dark Silas]]', whose husk was placed in Temporal Stasis by [[Carter Moonwing]]. The husk remains in the now-empty Well of Souls. As a consequence, the Souls in Temporal Stasis can no longer be communed with by the people of [[Astraloftum]], causing widespread concern.

## Inside the Tree
The interior chambers circle the main trunk and let moonlight down to the hidden [[Well of Souls]]. Chambers are numbered top to bottom:

1. **The Ruined Light-orb** — A former lighthouse, perforated by corrupted souls. Guarded by a corrupted Stone Guardian of Love's Fragment.
2. **The Wingless Library** — Where Soul knowledge was stored in Orbs of True Knowledge. The [[Oocoo]] were once stewards here before succumbing to corruption. Contains a chamber with a [[Rubix Cube|Rubix Cube]] readable only by [[Carter Moonwing]].
3. **Mouth of the Great** — The dungeon entrance. A living-root drawbridge connects to the Forsaken Shrine. Carvings depict [[Natu]] battling a massive evil eye.
4. **The Forsaken Shrine of Natu** — A desecrated shrine containing a statue of [[Natu]] missing a Love Fragment. Home of Squanatos the Necromancer Squirrel.
5. **The Tainted Lake & Village of the Lost** — A blood-tainted lake and the ruins of a squirrel colony. Sole survivor: Squanatos.
6. **Gas Pass** — Gassy tunnels filled with bubbles, acid rivers, and crimson thorns. Home to an Ankheg.
7. **Crimson Forest** — Blighted flora and fauna. A thicket of crimson thorns blocks the path to the [[Well of Souls]].
