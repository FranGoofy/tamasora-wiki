The Flying Holy City of Astraloftum is a divine bastion that drifts through the four [[Skysea|Skyseas]] and serves as the Capitol of the [[Ecclesia Solari]], worshippers of the [[Trinity]], and of the [[Solar Scions]]. Most of the [[Soul Shepherds]] family members live here and they consider it their home.

Lord Commander: [[Zariel Tenebris]]
High Priestess: [[High Priestess Cetra]]

## Districts & Locations

### [[St. Ode'bbej Cathedral]]
An enormous, towering, white cathedral that can be seen from anywhere in the city. This is where sermons are read, people pray, debate holy scripture, store ancient relics, and where the [[High Priestess Cetra|High Priestess]] and the rest of the Church Hierarchy reside.

- **[[Hall of Scions]]** — Headquarters of the [[Solar Scions]], where missions are handed out and Scions are tracked.
- **[[St. Ode'bbej Cathedral#St. Ode'bbej Square|St. Ode'bbej Square]]** — Central gathering point for the populace, home to the Tomb of Saint Ode'bbej, guarded by [[St. Chopin]].

### [[Whispering Grove]]
A sacred natural place where the druids of the Circle of [[Natu]] reside, protected by the druids and home to the celestial birds known as Avieres. Site of the [[Whispering Grove#Symphony of Ascension|Symphony of Ascension]] ritual.

- **[[Great Natu Tree]]** — An ancient tree over 300 years old, last of its kind from the forgotten [[Paradise]]. Houses the [[Well of Souls]].

### [[Solari Abbey]]
Nestled in the Magi Crags behind the cathedral, where Monks meditate and train. Home to the [[Solari Abbey#The Scriptorium|Scriptorium]] and [[Natu's Starlit Nest]].

### [[Forgehearth District]]
Where development of airships, holy automatons, and weaponry occurs. Home to [[Tekkhead Labs]] and [[Forgehearth District#The Forgotten Chamber|The Forgotten Chamber]].

### Aeritha District
A dazzling maze of blue and white buildings built below aqueducts that flow divine water. The majority of residents live here — a melting pot of [[Dwarf|Dwarves]], Halflings, Gnomes, [[Tiefling|Tieflings]], and Humans.

### [[Sun Docks]]
Where [[Solar Scions|Solar Sailships]] restore their energy using the radiant power of the Sun. Currently experiencing a refugee crisis from the [[Atlas Kingdoms]].

### [[Tower of Inquisition]]
Headquarters of the [[Solari Inquisition]], who serve as the City's Militia and Expeditionary Army. Said to contain a secret prison for [[Fomorians]] deep within.

## History

Astraloftum recently survived the devastating [[Fomorian Invasion of Astraloftum]], orchestrated by the [[Cult of Nirvana]]. The city was saved through the heroism of the [[Soul Shepherds]]. The attack on the [[Well of Souls]] within the [[Great Natu Tree]] was repelled, though at a cost — the souls within the tree were placed into Temporal Stasis and can no longer be communed with by the city's residents.
