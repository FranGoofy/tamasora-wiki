Located in [[Astraloftum]], the Tower of Inquisition is where the [[Solari Inquisition]] reside, train, live, and congregate. Inquisitors pore over ancient texts seeking signs of heresy and threats to the divine order. They also serve as the City's Militia and Expeditionary Army, operating under the direction of Lord Commander [[Zariel Tenebris]].

The [[Solari Inquisition]] does not have good relations with the [[Solar Scions]] — animosity between the two factions is common.

## Secrets
Legends speak of a secret prison hidden deep within the Tower, where [[Fomorians]] and those deemed tainted by Fomorian magic languish in eternal shadow.
