Located below the [[Forgehearth District]] Warehouse. A golden metal door lies at the end of the chamber, flanked by two angelic gargoyles. A Celestial inscription above reads: *"Here she slumbers awaiting Pralaya."* Three empty slots shaped like a sun, two half-circles, and an oval sit on a platform before the door.

The door features three carved panels:
- **Dawn** — A radiant woman [[Natu]] weaving primordial tree branches into a protective barrier against a tentacled creature, with a burgeoning city behind her. 
- **Zeal** — Students gathered around a Man of Faces [[Magi]] holding a tome etched with an hourglass symbol, arcane energy crackling as a volcano erupts outside.
- **Ruin** — A lone tattooed warrior standing sentinel on a celestial peak [[Tekk]], gripping a blacksmith's hammer, while monstrous figures below drag people into the void.

Past the golden metal door is the [[Ark of the Trinity]].
