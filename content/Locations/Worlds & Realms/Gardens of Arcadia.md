A hidden realm created by the Elven Demigod [[Amenó]], first-born son of [[Natu]], after a great calamity drove him and his [[Fey]] servants from [[Tamasora]]. It became a paradise of nature, poetry, song, and art — a perfect, unchanging realm where art is a mirror held to nature.

**\[Secret Lore\]** The entirety of the Gardens reside inside a 'Lifebook' created in secret by [[Amenó]] and overseen by 'the Librarian', a celestial entity imprisoned by the Gods, and enigmatic leader of the [[Scribes]].

[[Amenó]] was the first King and founder of the Court. His offspring separated into different factions and created the individual sub courts: The [[Court of Spring]] and Orchid Courts are one of the same, and then the others — Autumn, Summer, Winter.

Arcadia is a perfect, unchanging realm. Its denizens are melodramatic, quick to take offense, and hold grudges for ages. Adventurous Arcadians relish returning to [[Tamasora]], fascinated by what mortals have done with the world.

## Noble Houses & Courts

- **[[Court of Arcadia]]** — The ruling court, led by [[Amenó]].
- **[[Court of Spring]]** — Allied with the [[Court of Ash]] upon their arrival.
- **[[Court of Ash]]** — An extinct noble house, cursed by the [[Monarchon]].

## Notable Figures

- **[[Amenó]]** — Elven Demigod, first-born son of [[Natu]], founder and first King.
- **[[Monarchon]]** — Ruler of the Court of Arcadia; her daughter, the Spring Princess, has gone missing.
- **[[The Vizier]]** — Val's magus, most powerful mage in Arcadia.
- **[[Ash Marshal]]** — [[Amenó]]'s timescape hunter, regularly dispatched across the planes.

## The Missing Spring Princess
Ten Tamasoran years have passed since the disappearance of the [[Monarchon]]'s daughter, the Spring Princess. The Monarchon has vowed to leave no stone unturned until she is found. See also: [[Great Lacuna]].
