**Weastworld**, formerly known as **Looselaw**, was a small city located in the Aigean Sands of [[Aigora]]. Being located at a strategic gateway to the uncharted continent, it was a hotspot for adventurers, pirates, explorers, and individuals of illicit and sketchy occupations. 

The city used to be known as Looselaw and under the control and reign of [Crondusa](https://wind-of-the-weast.fandom.com/wiki/Crondusa "Crondusa")**, Queen of Teeth**, before she was overthrown by the [[Weastern Pirates]]. After taking control of the city and killing **Crondusa**, the Weastern Pirates renamed the city in their grand scope, **Weastworld**, with the intention of creating an "amusement park".
