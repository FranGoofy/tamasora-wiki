# The Battle of Spider Isle

*Chapter I Event — Early Campaign*

The **Battle of Spider Isle** was the first major conflict involving the newly formed [[Solar Scions]] group, later called the [[Soul Shepherds]]. It took place on [[Spider Island]] south of the Temple of Luminaria, where a [[Fomorians]] Succubus known as the **Servant of Nirvana** had been orchestrating a war between the Spider Queendom and [[Astraloftum]].

## Key Events
- **[[Animus Tenebris]]** discovered and defeated the [[Spider Queen]] before the Solar Scions arrived.
- Two Spider Princesses were being mind-controlled by the Fomorian Succubus via a mysterious "[[Twilight Brand]]."
- The Succubus revealed to [[Silas Clemens|Silas]] that he was "a failure of the Fomorians" and would never amount to being "the Unborn Fomor King."
- [[Diallos]] shattered the Succubus's **phylactery**, releasing tens of trapped Souls — all inadvertently absorbed by Silas, who fell unconscious.
- The [[Spider Princesses]] were liberated from mind control through the combined efforts of Silas (channeling the Spider Queen's Soul) and [[Whimsical]]'s motherly love.

## Aftermath
- Silas absorbed the Spider Queen's Soul.
- The Spider Princesses were crowned as new Queens in a coronation ceremony attended by the Solar Scions.
- [[Syrax]] the Ruby Dragon revealed to [[Diallos]] his destiny with the Gem Dragons of Paradise, dubbing him "Argahax" (Draconic for "Prince of the Comet").
- Syrax revealed the "[[Ruby Incident]]".
