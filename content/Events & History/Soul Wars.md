A devastating conflict among the gods concerning [[Souls]]. The [[Soul Wars]] were catalyzed by [[Magi]]'s descent into madness — his Split Soul creating a ceaseless internal conflict between destruction and enlightenment.

In the aftermath, [[Tekk]] proposed a transformative solution: offering mortals the chance to attain [[Souls]], dubbed 'Spirit Weapons'. He extended this to his own [[Automaton|constructs]] as well, paving the way for 'Artificial Souls'. The earliest and most potent of these sentient weapons were lost to the annals of time. The [[Ecclesia Solari]] forbids research on them, considering these artifacts too perilous.
