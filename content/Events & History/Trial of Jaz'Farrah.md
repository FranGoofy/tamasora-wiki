# The Trial of Jaz'Farrah

*Chapter V — The Shattering of the Soul Shepherds*

The **Trial of Jaz'Farrah** was the pivotal event at the end of the Saturn arc that irrevocably fractured the Soul Shepherds fellowship and set several major plot threads in motion.

## The Setup
After defeating [[Jaz'Farrah]] at the [[Elephant Spire]] and ending the 14th Time Loop, the Soul Shepherds escorted her to [[Janus City]] and delivered her to the [[Jas-Sharaza]] Council at [[Elefanti Palace]] for trial. The charges: Treason, Conspiracy, Magical Assault, Murder, and trafficking of the sacred [[Regalo Fruit]]. Her ward [[Talleah Preshons|Talleah]] voluntarily submitted to face judgment alongside her.

## The Secret Plan
The night before the trial, [[Carter Moonwing|Carter]] (revealed to be a Carter from an alternate timestream) confided in [[Silas Clemens|Silas]] that he needed Jaz'Farrah's help to return to his own timestream. Together they devised a secret escape plan, aware it would likely be their last act with the Soul Shepherds.

## The Trial
- **[[Reina Akaska]]** presented the evidence as "the Iron Feather."
- **[[Diallos]]** questioned Jaz'Farrah using Detect Thoughts, uncovering a traumatized soul who genuinely believes her methods are righteous — but who twisted his questions to sow discord.
- The Jas-Sharaza Council delivered a verdict: **execution** for both Jaz'Farrah and Talleah.

## The Escape
- [[Silas Clemens|Silas]] unleashed a shroud of **magical darkness**, plunging the courtroom into chaos.
- Silas used a **Scroll of Power Word: Peel** to tear a spatial rift in reality.
- [[Diallos]] and [[Characters/SxS Player Characters/Virtus Tenebris|Virtus]] were knocked into the rift by the chaos.
- **[[Finley]]**, standing resolute, intercepted Jaz'Farrah as she fled — striking her with an airborne blow and sending her spiraling into the rift.
- Carter and Jaz'Farrah escaped together, vanishing into the sky.
- Silas dispelled the rift before it could consume the world.
- Whimsical stoically ushered Council members to safety.
- Virtus watched Carter and Jaz'Farrah escape, powerless to stop it.

## Aftermath
- **[[Silas Clemens|Silas]] and [[Talleah Preshons|Talleah]]** were arrested and transported back to [[Astraloftum]] aboard the [[Project A.R.K.]] as prisoners.
- **[[Carter Moonwing|Carter]] and [[Jaz'Farrah]]** are at large. Carter is declared an [[Apostate]] by the Ecclesia Solari.
- **[[Thirteen]]** (SABER operative) is formally tasked with hunting Carter down.
- The Jas-Sharaza Council, despite the chaos, honored their debt to the Soul Shepherds and funded the full airship repair (35,000 gp).
- The Soul Shepherds departed Saturn with their fellowship shattered but their mission ongoing.
