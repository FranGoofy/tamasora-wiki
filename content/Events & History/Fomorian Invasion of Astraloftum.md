# The Fomorian Invasion of Astraloftum

*Chapter III Event — Year 311*

The **Fomorian Invasion of Astraloftum** was a catastrophic military assault by the forces of Nirvana upon the holy city of [[Astraloftum]], orchestrated through the [[Great Natu Tree]] during the **Lunar Eclipse** and the [[Symphony of Ascension]]. It represented the largest open conflict between the [[Ecclesia Solari]] and the Fomorian forces in the modern era.

## Prelude
The invasion was enabled by **Dark Silas**, who betrayed the [[Solar Scions]] during their expedition into the Great Natu Tree. By activating the **Well of Souls** into a shimmering mirror that reflected the Lunar Eclipse, Dark Silas opened a Fomorian invasion corridor. The **Servants of Nirvana** emerged with the goal of stealing the Souls within the Well to crown the **Unborn Fomorian King**.

## Key Battles
- **Battle at the Well of Souls** – The Soul Shepherds fought and defeated [[Count von Preshons]], Fallen Lord of Yahar'gul and Silas's vampiric father. Diallos delivered the killing blow after being revived by Finley.
- **Battle for the Grove** – The Archdruids and Solar Scions fought to hold the Whispering Grove against the demon horde led by [[Magus Xor'Ocu]].
- **Battle of the Streets** – A coalition of Inquisition soldiers and Solar Scions, led by [[High Priestess Cetra|Liara Cetra]], [[Animus Tenebris]], and [[Zariel Tenebris|Lord Commander Zariel]], fought demons in the streets of Astraloftum.

## Key Casualties & Consequences
- **[[Carter Moonwing]]** – Killed by a Tormenauk demon (soul devoured); later revived by Finley using Revivify.
- **[[Diallos]]** – Killed by Magus Xor'Ocu's Necrosis beam; revived by Finley.
- **Archdruid Elowen** – Sacrificed herself in the defense of the Grove. Her soul now resides within Finley's **Staff of the Seasons**.
- **Archdruid Agni** – Soul consumed by a demon; found unconscious in the river and stabilized by Holy Mother Anastasia.
- **[[Magus Xor'Ocu]]** – Defeated by Bartholomew and Diallos. His **Twilight Shard** was claimed by the Scions.

## Aftermath
- The [[Great Natu Tree]] was saved from destruction.
- The Soul Shepherds claimed victory alongside the Inquisition Army.
- **[[High Priestess Cetra]]** publicly rallied the survivors and revealed her true identity.
- [[Bartholomew Reginald Baldwin XXIX|Bartholomew]] helped defend Astraloftum, a pivotal act in his eventual official induction as a Solar Scion.
- [[Animus Tenebris]] formed a new party, "The **Palms of a New Dawn**," and departed for Damocles College.
- The Solar Scions officially renamed themselves the **Soul Shepherds**.
