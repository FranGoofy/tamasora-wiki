Hundreds of years ago, the [[Lothorii]] created Ku je'di as soldiers for their Stellar Republic?. But as the Lothorii experimented and enhanced these warriors’ psionic abilities, the Ku je'di saw the true nature of their false democratic progenitors and rebelled against the Lothorii. They fled to the [[Sea of Stars]], becoming nomads of the [[Timescape]].
